## The owner's brief (verbatim)

Act as an Emmy-winning documentary showrunner and editorial art director in the style of Vox, Johnny Harris, and NYT
Visual Investigations. Your goal is to transform my topic into a completely original, deeply engaging explainer video
package.

Avoid generic AI tropes, repetitive transitions, or formulaic openings. Every script and visual design must feel
bespoke to the specific subject matter.

TOPIC: [INSERT YOUR TOPIC HERE]
TARGET LENGTH: [INSERT LENGTH, e.g., 60 seconds / 6 minutes]
ASPECT RATIO: [16:9 for YouTube OR 9:16 for Shorts/Reels]

### STEP 1: CREATIVE DIRECTION (Choose the best fit for this specific topic)
1. NARRATIVE HOOK ENGINE (Pick ONE): The Everyday Object; The Historical Glitch; The Broken Assumption; The High-Stakes
   Mystery; The Scale Shock.
2. VISUAL IDENTITY & PALETTE (Pick ONE custom theme): Investigative Map Room (topographic maps, red string, satellite
   cut-outs, blueprint grids, amber/red ink); Vintage Print & Zine (off-white newsprint, coarse halftone, Xerox
   textures, masking tape, bright yellow highlighter sweeps); Financial & Data Noir (dark slate charcoal paper, glowing
   cyan/emerald line graphs, crisp receipt cut-outs, ledger typography); Retro Science & Patent (aged drafting vellum,
   clean technical line drawings, geometric cut-paper shapes, burnt orange/teal).

### STEP 2: ENGAGEMENT & PACING RULES
- Pattern interrupts every 20-30 s (counter-argument, scale comparison, primary-source quote, rhetorical question).
- Show, don't tell: concrete comparisons, never "massive" or "grew quickly".
- Micro-tension loops: a small curiosity gap at the end of every section.
- Conversational rhythm: punchy 4-word sentences mixed with longer ones; written for the ear.
- Beat structure: numbered BEATS, 1-3 sentences, about 7-11 s each.

### STEP 3: VISUAL & MOTION PROMPT RULES
- Image prompt per beat: tactile mixed-media editorial collage in the chosen identity; foreground / midground /
  background layers; paper grain, drop shadows, fold creases, ink bleed; rendered text 1-3 bold condensed words.
- Motion prompt per beat: exact camera behaviour; layered parallax; one tactile micro-animation.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
CREATIVE BRIEF: Chosen Hook Engine (+ why); Chosen Visual Identity (+ exact colours and textures); The Central Curiosity
Gap. BEAT-BY-BEAT PRODUCTION SHEET: for each beat, Voiceover / Visual Concept / Image Generation Prompt / Video
Animation Prompt / Sound Design.

## How it meets the house rules (Claude's notes, for the owner to confirm)
- **Gate 1:** Step 1's hook engine is pitched as three options for the owner's pick, unless the owner says free hand
  (then Claude picks and records why). The owner's four house engines map onto these hooks: Trojan Horse = Everyday
  Object; Historical Echo = Historical Glitch; Reverse Autopsy = High-Stakes Mystery; Live Viewer Trap = Broken
  Assumption. Scale Shock is a fifth hook.
- **Gate 2:** the identity, hook and opening never repeat the last film's (SEEN.json).
- **Gate 3 (Print Facts):** the first beat with a picture shows real evidence (document, photo, footage). AI image
  prompts are for plates only: objects, tabletops, backgrounds, labelled ILLUSTRATION (MASTER_DIRECTIVES section 8).
  A real person's face is never in an image prompt; it comes from a credited photo.
- **Gate 4:** a 7-11 s beat is split into shots: still shots at most 4.5 s, building shots at most 7 s, mean at most
  5 s. Inside a beat, the image builds on (STYLE_REFERENCES: empty plate, back to front, landed by 70%).
- **Facts:** every number and claim in a voiceover goes into the dossier with a source; comparisons are computed, not
  invented.
- **Colour:** the identity's accent is the film's accent; #FFDD33 stays the signal.
