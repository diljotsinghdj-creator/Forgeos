# Design kits (one per film, never shared by two films in a row)

Proposed 2026-11-13 after the owner's verdict on the politics samples ("both videos look same: fonts, typography,
chart, bars"). Each kit sets the whole surface: paper, palette, type pairing, how a number, a bar, a label and a
caption are drawn, the cut-out treatment and the props. The stills in `stills/` are the approval frames (HTML built
with the studio's own fonts and real assets, rendered in Chromium); once the owner approves, the kit becomes a studio
look and the variation gate compares kits, not only shot kinds and hue.

## Editorial shards (US midterms)
From the owner's references: the "2024" election collage and the Kupang poster.
- Paper: warm cream with fine grain. Palette: orange #E8551E, blue #2B57A8, black, signature yellow as highlighter.
- Shapes: large angular shards behind the people, a black ink stroke as the ground line.
- People and places: real photos, black-and-white halftone cut-outs with a white sticker edge and a hard offset shadow.
- Type: Playfair 900 for years and headlines (stacked, coloured), Inter 800 caps on black tags, Permanent Marker notes.
- Data: torn-paper bars in the palette, serif numerals sitting on them, the lost part as a dashed outline.

## Archive dossier (India SIR)
From the owner's references: the ledger-paper political collage and the Monument Teller poster.
- Paper: aged ledger (ruled lines, red margin, coffee rings, grain). Palette: ink red #B3261E, black, kraft, warning
  yellow (signature).
- Real documents taped on, tilted; rubber stamps (Anton, roughened, multiply); warning tape; index-card titles.
- Type: JetBrains Mono (typewriter) for labels and files, Anton for stamps and the big number, Caveat red pen notes.
- Data: a typed roll page with rows struck through in red pen; the figure as a giant Anton numeral with a typed
  caption. Any recreated page is labelled ILLUSTRATIVE and carries no real names.
