NEWS SHORT IN 30 MINUTES - Print Facts (Hindi)

Your two best Shorts (1.2k and 1.3k views) reacted to that day's political news. This turns a fresh story into a
40-second Hindi Short fast. Post within a few hours of the news, 6-9 pm IST.

MINUTE 0-10: PICK AND CHECK
  1. Pick ONE story trending today (Rahul Gandhi, Election Commission, Modi/Shah, Delhi protests did best).
  2. Open 2 news reports on it. Copy their links. Note 1 number or 1 exact quote.
  3. Write down the other side's response (the EC, the BJP, the government). One line is enough.

MINUTE 10-20: FILL THE TEMPLATE (TEMPLATE_News_Short_Hindi.txt)
  Replace every [BRACKET]. Keep each spoken line under 12 words. 7 lines total, about 80 words.
    Line 1  HOOK      the most shocking fact or quote, said first. Under 8 words. Tone: dramatic
    Line 2  WHO       who said/did it, and where. Real name (CreatorForge shows their real photo).
    Line 3  WHAT      the exact quote or number - on screen as Quote:/Chart:, cited
    Line 4  WHY NOW   one line of context: what led to this
    Line 5  OTHER SIDE the response (keeps you fair and safe)  Tone: calm
    Line 6  STAKES    why it matters to the viewer  Tone: strong
    Line 7  QUESTION  ask the viewer + point to the long video  Tone: question
  Put both news links in Source: - the Article: line and any newspaper picture use the REAL page.

MINUTE 20-30: MAKE AND POST
  CreatorForge -> Generate -> Script -> paste. 9:16 Shorts, Fast cuts, voice "Hindi news anchor male ★", pace 1.1x.
  Title formula (Hinglish, under 60 characters):   🔥 [Name] Ka [Shock Word]! [Short question]?
     e.g.  🔥 Rahul Gandhi Ka Gyanesh Kumar Par Hamla! 30 Second Mein Khatam?
  Description: the two source links. Related video: link your long video on the same story.
  Pinned comment: "पूरी कहानी: [long video link]"

RULES THAT KEEP THE CHANNEL SAFE
  - A claim is said as a claim ("राहुल का आरोप", "आयोग का कहना"), never as a proven fact.
  - Always one line of the other side.
  - Never another channel's video clip (copyright strikes made some of your Shorts private). Use real photos,
    article screenshots and Quote: cards of posts - CreatorForge makes those automatically.
