PHONE SHOOT LIST - LONG FORM 05, 06, 07
Simple faceless shots to film at home on a phone. Each one replaces the pictures for the spoken line quoted.
General: 16:9 landscape, phone on a stack of books or a cheap tripod, daylight from a window, no faces, no
readable names, card numbers or bank details (cover them or turn them over). Hold each shot still for about
3 s unless a move is described. Film 2-3 takes of each.


05  THE BUSINESS OF SELLING YOU FEAR

1. "People paid more for less insurance. Why?"
   Props: one thin leaflet, one thick folder, a few coins. Top-down on a table: thin leaflet left, thick folder
   right. A hand places the coins on the THIN one. 3 s.

2. "One word did that to a price."
   Props: a printed page (any letter, text out of focus), a red pen. Close, slightly angled: the pen draws a circle
   round one word. Keep the focus on the pen tip so the words stay soft. 3 s.

3. "So Maya puts the phone face down and waits until morning."
   Props: a phone, a lamp-lit table at night. Close, side angle: a hand turns the phone face down and slides it
   away. Optional second clip: the same table in morning light. 3 s each.

4. "Tonight's plan costs eight pounds a month."
   Props: eight one-pound coins (or any coins), squared paper. Top-down: a hand stacks the coins one by one. 3 s.

5. "Over two years, a hundred and ninety-two pounds."
   Props: 24 small envelopes or folded paper squares. Top-down: the rows already laid out, a slow push-in with the
   phone (or lift the phone straight up). 3 s.

6. "She had never added them up. Have you?"
   Props: a scrap of paper, a pencil, a calculator, a mug of tea. Close, top-down: the pencil hovers over an empty
   sum line and stops. 3 s.

7. "So she asks the four calm questions."
   Props: four blank index cards, a pen, a cup of coffee. Top-down: a hand lays the four cards in a row, one at a
   time. 3-4 s.

8. "Then the fear was the product."
   Props: any small boxed gadget, a handwritten price tag. Close, eye level on a shelf: the box sits alone, the
   tag in soft focus. Hold still. 3 s.


06  WHY SMART PEOPLE FALL FOR SCAMS

1. "Watch the number on his screen."
   Props: a phone set to ring (use a timer or alarm), a kitchen table at night. Close, top-down: the phone lights
   up and buzzes on the table; keep the screen slightly out of focus so nothing is readable. 3 s.

2. "It matches the one on the back of his card."
   Props: a bank card turned over with a sticky note covering the details. Close, top-down: a finger taps the back
   of the card. Never show real numbers. 3 s.

3. "It's on your pulse."
   Props: none. Very close, side angle: two fingers pressed to the inside of a wrist. 3 s.

4. "He hangs up. Not rude. He is breaking the script."
   Props: a phone. Close, top-down: a thumb presses the end-call button and the screen goes dark. 3 s.

5. "Here's the shortcut in the UK. Three digits. One five nine."
   Props: a sticky note with 159 written in marker, a fridge door. Close, eye level: a hand sticks the note on the
   fridge and smooths it down. 3 s.

6. "So use a different phone, or wait a few minutes before calling."
   Props: a kitchen timer or an egg timer. Close, side angle: a hand twists the timer to a few minutes and lets go.
   3 s.

7. "So the pause is your most valuable defence."
   Props: a cup of tea, a windowsill. Close, side angle: steam rising from the cup in window light. Hold still. 3 s.

8. "Today, save one five nine in your phone."
   Props: a phone. Close, over the shoulder (no face): a thumb tapping a new contact; screen soft-focused. 3 s.


07  THE MAN WHO SOLD THE EIFFEL TOWER

1. "That silence was never luck. It was designed into the deal."
   Props: an old-looking sheet of paper (tea-stained), a fountain pen or any pen. Top-down, warm lamp light: the pen
   lies across the paper. Slow push-in. 3 s. (Black-and-white looks right here.)

2. "And the same design is in scam messages people receive every day."
   Props: a phone, a kitchen table. Top-down: the phone lights up with a new message, screen soft-focused. 3 s.

3. "He even dressed like money."
   Props: a tie, a watch, a pair of gloves. Top-down on a dark cloth: a hand lays them out one by one. 3 s.

4. "The deal had to stay secret, to avoid a public outcry."
   Props: a curtain. Close, from inside the room: a hand draws the curtain across a bright window. 3 s.

5. "Notice what that one rule does. Nobody could check with anyone else."
   Props: a bundle of letters or envelopes, a small padlock or a rubber band. Close: a hand snaps the padlock (or
   band) round the bundle. 3 s.

6. "Out came two real notes. One had been hidden inside beforehand."
   Props: a small wooden or cardboard box, two banknotes. Close, side angle: two notes slide out of the box onto
   the table. 3 s.

7. "The rope was made of bedsheets."
   Props: a white bedsheet. Close, top-down: two corners knotted together, hands pulling the knot tight. 3 s.

8. "Hang up. Find the real number on your card, or the official website."
   Props: a phone, a bank card with the details covered by a sticky note. Top-down: thumb ends a call, then the
   hand turns the card over. 3-4 s.
