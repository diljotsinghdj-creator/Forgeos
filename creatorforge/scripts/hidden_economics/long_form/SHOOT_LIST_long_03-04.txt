PHONE SHOOT LIST - LONG FORM 03 and 04
Simple faceless shots to film at home on a phone. Each one replaces the pictures for ONE spoken line (quoted
exactly). Film in landscape (16:9), daylight from a window, phone locked on exposure, hold still or one slow
push-in. No faces, no readable brand names; turn any screens or papers so the text cannot be read.

03  WHY WE BUY THINGS WE CANNOT COMFORTABLY AFFORD

1. "Steady job, modest savings, never thought of themselves as a big spender."
   Hand drops three coins one by one into a jam jar on a windowsill. Close, side-on, 4 s.

2. "Half the bidders would pay cash. Half would pay by card."
   Two hands on a table: left fans out a few banknotes, right lays down a plain bank card. Top-down, 3 s.

3. "A six-hundred-pound watch, at fifty pounds a month, split across a year."
   Twelve small envelopes laid in a row on the kitchen table, a wristwatch placed at the end. Top-down, slow
   slide along the row, 4 s. (The Chart graphic plays on this line, so this shot is optional.)

4. "Fifty feels manageable. Six hundred might not."
   A long till receipt unrolled across the table until it runs off the edge, figures out of focus. Close,
   low angle along the paper, 3 s.

5. "Second, what is the total cost? Monthly figure, times months, plus any fees."
   Pencil adding up figures on the back of an envelope beside a calculator, numbers unreadable. Top-down, 4 s.

6. "So Sam closes the tab."
   Hand turns a phone face down on a windowsill. Close, side-on, 2-3 s.

7. "Fifty pounds a month stays put, every month for a year."
   One coin dropped into a glass jar already holding a few coins. Close, eye level with the jar, 3 s.

8. "Try the test today, on the last three things you bought."
   Three receipts laid out in a row with a pencil, hand taps each in turn, text unreadable. Top-down, 3 s.

04  HOW FREE APPS TURN YOUR ATTENTION INTO A BUSINESS

1. "At eleven at night, she opens an app for one minute."
   Dark bedroom, bedside clock showing 11:00, a hand picks up a phone that lights the pillow (screen turned
   away). Close, low on the bedside table, 3 s.

2. "She paid nothing. So who earned from the other nineteen?"
   Nineteen matchsticks laid in a neat row on a bedside table, an empty wallet open beside them. Top-down, 3 s.

3. "For another, alerts were off and phones put away."
   Hand slides a phone into a desk drawer and pushes the drawer shut. Close, side-on, 3 s.

4. "It offered people tools to cap their own future screen time."
   Hand turns the dial of a kitchen timer on a desk. Close, 3 s.

5. "Second, move your most distracting app off the home screen."
   Hand lifts a biscuit tin from the counter onto the top shelf of a cupboard. Medium, from below, 3 s.

6. "Notice her phone. It lights up far less at night."
   Phone lying dark and face down on a bedside table beside a glass of water, lamp off. Close, static, 3 s.

7. "It is time you chose."
   Breakfast table in morning sun, a hand pulls out a chair and sets down a mug. Medium, side-on, 3 s.
   (The Statement card plays on this line; use the shot just before or after it if preferred.)

8. "How? Seven days, three settings, about ten minutes to set up."
   Pencil ticking a short paper checklist on a wooden table in morning light, writing unreadable. Top-down, 3 s.
