How Scammers Create Urgency
On-screen hook: YOUR MONEY ISN'T SAFE?
Title: How Scammers Create Urgency: The Safe Account Script
Tags: #HiddenEconomics #scams #fraud #safeaccount #takefive
Source: Take Five to Stop Fraud (UK Finance), https://www.takefive-stopfraud.org.uk/; Crimestoppers UK, Protecting yourself against financial fraud, https://crimestoppers-uk.org/keeping-safe/fraud/protecting-yourself-against-financial-fraud

A scammer wants your money, but first they need your speed.
Visual: A stopwatch ticking on a dark table beside a phone, harsh side light
Text: SPEED FIRST

Here is a common script, told as a fictional example.
Visual: A phone screen showing a generic incoming message, text unreadable
Text: FICTIONAL EXAMPLE

Your account has been compromised. Criminals are draining it right now.
Visual: Close-up of a banking app balance on a phone, digits blurred, red tint
Text: "YOUR ACCOUNT IS AT RISK"

Move everything to a safe account before it is gone.
Visual: Extreme close-up of a thumb over a transfer button, screen text unreadable
Text: "MOVE IT TO A SAFE ACCOUNT"

Every sentence is designed to stop you checking.
Visual: A hand frozen above a phone keypad, a clock blurred in the background
Stock: hand hesitating over phone

But there is a detail that breaks the whole script.
Visual: Wide shot of a quiet kitchen at night, one lamp on, phone on the table

Banks and police do not ask you to move money to a safe account.
Visual: Close-up of a bank card and a phone lying side by side, screens dark
Text: NO REAL "SAFE ACCOUNT" REQUEST

That account belongs to the scammer.
Visual: A shadowed figure from behind walking away down a dim corridor
Text: IT'S THEIR ACCOUNT

A real problem will still be there in ten minutes.
Visual: An hourglass on a windowsill, sand still running, dawn light outside
Stock: hourglass on table

Hang up, then call your bank using a number you look up yourself.
Visual: Hands holding a phone at a kitchen table in calm morning light
Text: HANG UP. CALL BACK. VERIFY.

They needed your speed. So give them none of it.
Visual: The stopwatch from the opening, now stopped, lying beside a face-down phone
Text: TAKE YOUR TIME
