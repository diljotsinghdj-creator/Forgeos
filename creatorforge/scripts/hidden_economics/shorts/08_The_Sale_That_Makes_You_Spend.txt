Then a voucher appeared. Ten percent off a sixty-pound item.

So why did spending fifty-four feel like winning?
Visual: Close-up of a thumb tapping a glowing buy button, a small burst of light on the screen
Text: WHY DOES IT FEEL LIKE WINNING?

Economist Richard Thaler called it transaction utility, the pleasure of the deal itself.
Visual: An old economics journal open on a desk under a lamp, pages unreadable
Text: TRANSACTION UTILITY · THALER, 1985

The voucher sells you the deal. The deal sells you the item.
Visual: A red sale label peeled half off a plain cardboard box, macro shot
Text: VOUCHER → DEAL → ITEM

So what does the receipt never print?

The plan you started with, spending nothing.

Against that plan, you did not save six. You spent fifty-four.
