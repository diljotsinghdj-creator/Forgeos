E - VOICEOVER DIRECTION
Israel 2026: The Election That Settled Nothing

VOICE
  English narrator, mid-range, warm and dry. Think a well-read friend explaining over coffee, not an anchor.
  If you use ElevenLabs: a calm documentary voice. Use a voice you own or a library voice licensed for commercial use.
  No cloned voice of any real person, and no voice that imitates a known presenter or comedian.

PACE
  CreatorForge draft voice: voice speed 0.9 (≈135 words a minute) gives a ~48 min film.
  ElevenLabs: Stability 55-65, Similarity 75, Style 10-20, Speaker boost on. Generate one file per render part (R1-R6),
  so each part can be synced with voiceover_sync.
  Leave ~0.4 s between lines and ~1.5 s at each Chapter card. Let the last line of each chapter breathe (1 s).

TONE TAGS IN THE SCRIPT (each spoken line has a Tone: line)
  calm      - even, explanatory. Most lines.
  question  - lift the end, a small smile in the voice.
  strong    - firmer, slower on the key number or the punchline's last word.
  dramatic  - slower, lower, pause BEFORE the key phrase.
  Part 4 is calm and strong only. No smile in the voice anywhere in Part 4.

COMEDY DELIVERY
  Deadpan. Never laugh at your own line. Punchlines are thrown away, a beat after the setup:
    "...they may honestly consider [beat] a holiday."
    "...the opposition [beat] with better furniture."
    "...not just the door, but the window, [beat] and the cat flap."
  Do not lean on the jokes. If a line reads as a joke only because of the voice, it isn't a joke.

ACCURACY RULES FOR THE VOICE
  Read every line exactly as written - the words were checked against the evidence register (F).
  If you must change a word for rhythm, do not change a number, a name, a date, a quote, or "allegedly/reported".
