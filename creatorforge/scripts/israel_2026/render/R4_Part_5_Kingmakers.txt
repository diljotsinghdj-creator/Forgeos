To understand why the word Zionist carries so much weight, you need a bit of history. For most of Israel's existence, the Arab parties have been treated as outside the coalition game. The closest they came was in 1992, when Arab parties backed Yitzhak Rabin's government from outside, which helped make the Oslo peace process possible. His opponents never let him forget it.

It's worth hearing the Haredi case on its own terms. For their communities, full-time Torah study is a central religious value, and they argue it protects the Jewish people as surely as an army does. Many Haredi families also fear the army would pull young men away from their way of life. Those are sincere beliefs, held by more than a million Israelis.
