WORKFLOW
  1. Render R1-R6 on the pod (pod_israel_longform.sh does all six, 16:9, YouTube Documentary, Investigative desk).
  2. In the app's storyboard review for each part: check every auto-matched real photo (right person? real photo,
     not AI or a caricature? licence shown?). Replace anything doubtful with a generic picture.
  3. Optional ElevenLabs: one MP3 per part; run voiceover_sync per part (the script does this if you pass the MP3s).
  4. Join the six parts in order (ffmpeg concat, or your editor). The pod script writes a concat file and a joined MP4.
  5. Music, colour and final mix in your editor (CapCut / Resolve / Premiere). CreatorForge exports CapCut projects.

PACING
  Satire chapters: new picture every ~2 s (fast cuts on). Punchline lines: hold the last picture 0.5 s past the line.
  Part 4: set fast cuts OFF for R3 if you render it separately (3-4 s per picture). The pod script does this.
  Chapter cards: 3 s, title only, a soft whoosh, no music hit.
  Recaps (the halftime and Part 5 openers): reuse the same calculator and pie graphic for recognition.

SOUND
  Music bed at -24 LUFS under VO (VO -14 LUFS integrated, loudnorm two-pass already in voiceover_sync).
  Part 4: drop the music to a single sustained pad or silence; no SFX stings; room tone only.
  Light SFX in satire chapters only: page flip, calculator click, a stamp. Never a laugh track, never a rimshot.

GRAPHICS CHECKS (do before export)
  - Seat numbers on every Chart/Pie match F rows 3-4 (re-check certified results first).
  - Every number on screen has a Cite: line under it in the script; the source shows small at the bottom.
  - "ALLEGATION · NOT PROVEN" card stays on screen for the full Likud-claim line.
  - ILLUSTRATION corner label on all stick-figure scenes and any generated image.
  - Content advisory card at the top of Part 4 ("This chapter contains descriptions of war").

CAPTIONS
  English burnt-in captions ON (CreatorForge captions). Also upload the SRT so YouTube auto-translation works.
  Check spelling of names in captions against E's pronunciation list.

EXPORT
  1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264 high, 16-20 Mbps, AAC 320 kbps. Keep the project and each part's job folder until 30 days
  after upload, in case a correction is needed.

CORRECTIONS POLICY
  Corrections go in the pinned comment and the description with a timestamp. A material error (wrong number, wrong
  attribution) gets an on-screen correction card via YouTube's editor (blur/trim) or a re-upload if needed.
