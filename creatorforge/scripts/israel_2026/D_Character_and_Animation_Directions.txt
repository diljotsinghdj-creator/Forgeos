D - CHARACTER AND ANIMATION DIRECTIONS
Israel 2026: The Election That Settled Nothing

PRINCIPLE
  Real people appear ONLY as real, credited photographs. Animation is used for ideas, never for real individuals.
  Stick figures are anonymous ("a man", "two people") - they never wear a real politician's face, name tag, or
  signature costume, and they never appear in the Gaza / West Bank chapter.

LOOK
  Template: YouTube Documentary (youtube_longform), 16:9, Visual style: Investigative desk, Faceless, Fast cuts.
  Graphics palette: off-white paper, ink black, one accent red (seat 61, red lines), muted blue for the Knesset.
  Stick-figure scenes: "explainer" style, white background, no colour except the accent red on props.

THE EIGHT ANIMATED GAGS (the Animate: lines in the script, in order)
  1. Cold open - two people sit at a table, turn their chairs away, fold their arms. (Coalition talks in one image.)
  2. Cold open - musical chairs: music stops, everyone stands, nobody has a chair. (Seat 61 set-up.)
  3. Part 1 - a man erases numbers from a blackboard, another looks shocked. (Disqualify-the-votes letter.)
     Note: the eraser is the joke, not the voters. Do not show any Arab-coded character.
  4. Part 2 - a man writes, crumples, throws, starts again. (Netanyahu political obituaries.)
  5. Part 2 - a man sits proudly in a big chair that slowly sinks. (Largest bloc that isn't a majority.)
  6. Part 3 - a man closes a door, a window, and a small hatch, dusts off his hands. (Lieberman's red line.)
  7. Part 5 - five people in a circle each point at another and shake their heads. (Red lines.)
  8. Part 5 - a man tries three keys in a door, then sits on the floor. (Every key welded shut.)
  (Optional ninth: the Seat 61 runner can use Animate: "a torch beam searches an empty theatre".)

RUNNING VISUAL MOTIFS (graphics, not characters)
  - SEAT 61: a single empty theatre seat with a red tag "61". Appears in the cold open, in the Part 3 maths,
    in the halftime recap and as the final image ("61 · STILL MISSING"). Same framing every time.
  - THE CALCULATOR: a pocket calculator showing 53 / 48 / 19 - never 61. Used at each recap.
  - RED TAPE: every red line is literally a strip of red tape across a doorway on that leader's slide.
  - THE CLOCK: Herzog's mandate countdown (28 + 14 days) as an hourglass; reused in Part 7.

TONE BY CHAPTER
  Cold open, 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 - dry, satirical; jokes aimed at the powerful, at procedure and at hypocrisy.
  4 (Gaza / West Bank) - NO jokes, NO stick figures, NO sound-effect stings. Slow cuts (3-4 s per picture),
    candles, empty places, objects. Music drops to a single sustained tone or silence.
  7 - neutral, explanatory. 8 - quiet, the only callback joke is the last line.

COMEDY RULES (from the directive, applied)
  1. Punch up: politicians, parties, procedures, hypocrisy. Never punch at victims, voters, ethnic or religious groups.
  2. No joke within 30 seconds of a casualty figure, in any chapter.
  3. No jokes about the Holocaust, October 7 victims, hostages or Gaza's dead. Ever.
  4. Haredi and Arab citizens: their politics can be analysed; their identity is never the joke.
  5. Every joke must be true underneath: the gag only exaggerates a sourced fact (e.g. Bennett relied on Ra'am, now says
     he won't rely on Arab parties).
  6. No impressions, no mock accents, no fake quotes. Quotes are real or not used.
  7. Our own voice, not a borrowed one: no catchphrases, desk-bit structure, or segment names from existing shows.
