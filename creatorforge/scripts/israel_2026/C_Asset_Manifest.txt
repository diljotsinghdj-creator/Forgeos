Israel 2026: The Election That Settled Nothing

HOW PICTURES ARE MADE IN THIS FILM
  1. Visual: lines (334 in the script) - CreatorForge finds or makes one picture per line.
     - Named people, parties, buildings and institutions (Netanyahu, Knesset, ICC, Herzog...) are matched by name to a
       REAL photo on Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons (automatic). Licence: mostly CC BY / CC BY-SA or public domain.
       Credit each one in the description (CreatorForge writes the credits list into the job's provenance file).
     - Generic subjects (a ballot box, a calculator, an empty chair) come from stock (Pixabay/Unsplash/Pexels licences:
       free for commercial use, no credit required, credit given anyway) or are generated as illustrations.
  2. Stock: lines (4) - real footage searches: Israel polling station; Gaza destruction aerial; Tel Aviv protest night;
     Hostages Square Tel Aviv. Licence: Pixabay Content Licence / Wikimedia (check each clip's page) / Internet Archive (PD).
  3. Graphics (Chart, Pie, Timeline, Steps, Quote, Split, Text, Big, Statement, Icons, Receipt, Map) - drawn by
     CreatorForge from the script's own words and numbers. Original work, owned by you.
  4. Animate: lines (8) - generic stick figures drawn by CreatorForge. Original, no real person depicted.
  5. Archive: lines (2) - public-domain archive photos from Wikimedia Commons when one matches.

HARD RULES (apply in the storyboard review before rendering)
  - NO AI-generated face of any real person. If an auto-matched "real photo" of a politician is actually AI-made or
    a caricature, replace it. Never generate a politician's likeness.
  - NO cartoon, sticker, or animation composited over a real person's face.
  - Party logos: only for identification (e.g. on the seat chart), credited, never altered or mocked.
  - No real logos of companies, media brands or NGOs except as identification next to their own quote/claim.
  - Gaza / Lebanon imagery: no identifiable dead or injured people, no children's faces. Use places and objects.
  - Hostage imagery: no hostage faces or posters unless from an official family forum release for press use.
  - Every illustration (AI image, stick-figure animation, reconstruction) gets the corner label ILLUSTRATION.
  - YouTube "altered or synthetic content" switch: ON if any realistic AI image remains in the final cut.

FOOTAGE NEVER TO USE
  - Hamas or other propaganda videos, hostage videos, or footage of killings.
  - Footage of the dead or wounded, or identifiable children in Gaza.
  - News channel footage (Channel 12, Al Jazeera, i24, etc.) without a licence - cite their reporting, don't show their video.
  - Any AI-generated "photo" of a real event.
