DESCRIPTION (paste into YouTube)
130 million names have been cut from India's draft voter lists. Opposition leaders have been briefly
arrested marching on the Election Commission. Delhi has shut metro stations ahead of protests.

This documentary follows five pieces of evidence: the deleted names, the 2023 law that changed who picks
India's election commissioners, Reuters' count of opposition politicians targeted by the Enforcement
Directorate, India's 157th place for press freedom, and the democracy ratings from Freedom House and V-Dem.
It also covers the evidence on the other side: the 2024 election, when the BJP fell short of a majority,
and the Supreme Court striking down electoral bonds.

The Election Commission says the deletions remove dead, duplicate and shifted voters, and that wrongly
deleted voters can re-enrol. The government denies political interference and rejects the international
rankings. Allegations of deliberate exclusion are disputed and have not been proven in court.
