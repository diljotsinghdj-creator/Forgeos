# Can I Give You a False Memory in 60 Seconds?

- **Channel:** Dragon Psychology, in DJ style (John Oliver voice, Vox × Johnny Harris visuals).
- **Length:** about 9:52 · 1,433 spoken words · 155 rows (one row is one shot; the 15 list words are three rows).
- **Status:** draft 1, for the owner's approval. No video until "go".
- **Voice:** Kokoro `af_heart` at 1.1 until the owner picks another.
- **Facts:** every number is in `DOSSIER.md` and is tagged in the Visuals column, like [D8]. ⚠ marks what is confirmed against the original paper before the voice is recorded.
- **Film accent (the one allowed):** sign red #C53030, used only on the road signs and the parked car in the car clip and its test card, and as the study desk's red-ink marker. Everything else is slate #141824, lab-paper #F0F4F8, cobalt #2B6CB0, amber #ED8936.
- **Canvas (backbone):** the brain's intake desk. A slate desktop with a lab-paper in-tray, index cards, carbon-copy forms and one rubber stamp that reads RECEIVED. Clippings, photos, study cards and charts slide onto it with hard drop shadows. Cut-out silhouettes only; no faces.
- **Timecodes** are estimates (speech at about 154 words a minute, plus the fixed list, the clip and every hold). Anything shown on screen as a timecode is set from the final cut.

## How the two traps work
1. **Trap 1 (word list):** fifteen words read alone, one per 1.4 s card, from the D2 SLEEP list. "Sleep" is never said or shown before the test.
2. A thin amber bar starts on the first word (0:18) and runs 60 s to the test (1:17). It is a bar, never a clock face.
3. The test asks DREAM (on the list), DOOR (not on it), SLEEP (never said). Viewers answer out loud before the reveal (1:34).
4. **Trap 2 (car clip, 0:42):** a 5 s paper cut-out clip shows a blue car pass a red-bordered inverted-triangle GIVE WAY sign and bump a parked red car. No stop sign exists anywhere in the clip.
5. The narrator says "stop sign" twice in passing: plant 1 at about 0:49, plant 2 at about 6:36. Never on screen, never stressed, read at the speed of the words around it.
6. The test at about 8:29 offers an octagon STOP and a triangle GIVE WAY. The replay shows the clip with its timecode, then both "stop sign" lines as text with their own timecodes.
