## House rules followed
- The channel palette, no yellow, no logo, no mascot, no host.
- **No faces drawn, no AI.** People are cut-out silhouettes. The two real people appear once, in one credited photo, framed
  neutrally, with nothing added to them (50). The other man in the case is not named or shown. The 1984 crime is not depicted.
- **Every study has a study card** (authors, year, journal, sample size where it is confirmed). REPLICATED and DISPUTED stamps
  appear only where the dossier supports them (40, 41, 56).
- **Bars start at zero.** Where the dossier holds a direction but no value (15, 51, 52), the chart shows no numbers and says
  "illustrative" or "schematic".
- **A source line on every frame,** bottom left.
- **Nothing is invented.** Handwriting is a stand-in of loops with no words (31, 36, 38), so no student's answer and no childhood
  story is made up. Reconstructed documents say RECONSTRUCTION.
- **The trap's own rules hold:** the word "sleep" appears nowhere before frame 11; no sign is named on screen before frame 53;
  the sign in the clip is the give-way triangle in both 06a and 54.
- **Checked:** every frame was looked at on the sheets, and `qa_edges.py` finds nothing cut off at the frame edge (the four
  pieces it lists are a podium, a desk and a stack that leave the frame on purpose).

## Things to know
1. **Real material is in 32% of frames, under the 60% target (MASTER_DIRECTIVES §8c).** There is no AI anywhere. The reason is
   the subject: the first two minutes are a word test, and no archive footage of a word list exists. What would raise it:
   photographed stationery in place of drawn (a real index card, a real stamp, a real in-tray), real road-sign photos on the test
   card (53), a period television for the Challenger beat.
2. **One licence needs your call.** The photo of Jennifer Thompson-Cannino and Ronald Cotton (50) is CC BY-SA 2.0. It is credited
   on the frame. Share-alike is a different condition from the CC BY and CC0 photos used elsewhere. Without it, frame 50 becomes
   the book's title card alone.
3. **Four photos come from 1,024-pixel files** (the courthouse, the lecture hall, the mall, the balloon). They are shown as
   prints on the desk with a halftone screen, never full frame. NASA's launch photo is full quality.
4. **Some study cards show no sample size yet** (13, 15, the 1995 card in 22, 41, 51, 56). They get one when the number is
   confirmed against the paper.
5. **The list frames (04a–e) show the first five words in the order the dossier has them.** That order is still to confirm
   against the 1995 paper.
6. **The courthouse** is the historic Alamance County Courthouse in Graham. The case was tried in that county; which building
   the 1985 trial used is not confirmed, so the frames name the courthouse and the county and claim no more.
7. **Five frames are sight gags** (07, 18, 25, 35, 44). They are studio drawings and say so.
8. **Three frames are plain on purpose:** 11 (one word), 29 (73 seconds, in silence) and 59 (sources).
9. **Not made yet:** the motion sheets (how each frame builds), the thumbnail, the voice, the video.
