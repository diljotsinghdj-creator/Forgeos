# The Buttons That Do Nothing (working title): script, draft 1

- **Channel:** Dragon Psychology, long form, 16:9, about 10½ minutes.
- **Engine:** A, *Live Viewer Trap* (owner's pick, 2026-11-19; recorded in `pick.json`). The film asks the viewer to "press" a big
  amber button in their head; three lamps "answer". The `[TRAP REVEAL]` (act 3, at the midpoint) opens the panel: the button
  was never wired, and the lamps ran on a timer. The button returns as a callback in acts 3–5 and pays off at the end (the one
  connected button was the viewer's own pause button). This follows `[SIMULATE]` in VOX_PRODUCTION_SYSTEM.md: trick live first,
  then reveal the mechanism.
- **Structure and voice:** the Vox × Johnny Harris five beats (hook and question → map and history → mechanics → stakes →
  synthesis), in the John Oliver voice: fact → receipt → simile → snap back. Written for motion (MASTER_DIRECTIVES §10b):
  deictic lines, a zoom-out hook, 4–7-word punchlines against 15–20-word explanations.
- **Canvas backbone:** one control panel on slate all film (`[AI·1]` base plate, finished in the studio): the big amber button
  and three lamps, a crosswalk box, a lift panel, an office thermostat, toggle switches, two dials, a small map screen and a side
  clip for study cards. Nothing hard-cuts away from it; the panel face swings open for the reveal. New for the bank: no clock,
  no countdown, no study desk, no pressure valve, no membership card (Prophecy's kit).
- **Palette:** slate #141824, lab-paper #F0F4F8, cobalt #2B6CB0 for structure, amber #ED8936 as the only signal colour (the
  button, the lamps, key numbers, the highlighter). No yellow. No extra film accent.
- **People:** stick figures and black-and-white silhouettes only. No real faces; the footballers on Langer's tickets are black
  silhouettes. Officials and researchers are named only as sources, never mocked.
- **Satire target:** systems, documents and official language (city figures, lift standards, trade-press polls), and the brain
  as an over-eager middle manager. Never a named person's character. No John Oliver catchphrases or bits.
- **Allegations and the other side:** every quote is attributed ("the Times quoted…", "TfL told the BBC…"). The other side gets
  its own rows: London's buttons mostly work; the lift industry says close buttons work after the wait; the thermostat poll is
  informal; Langer's effect is smaller than the textbook version; placebo control may help with stress; and new accessible
  buttons really do work.
- **Numbers and facts:** all are in `dossier.json` with a source and a status. ⚠ marks an item to check before voice. Charts
  and the map screen are drawn by the studio from the dossier, bars from zero; AI never draws a number.
- **Bracket notes:**
  - `[CUT]`: a 0.2 s music hard cut before a punchline, the only allowed hard cut.
  - `[HOLD 0.6s]`: a pause after a reveal.
  - `[WAIT n s]`: a silent beat of the viewer trap, timed exactly (the lamps are keyed to these, not to the viewer).
  - `[AI·n]`: an AI plate (flat paper collage, DP palette, no faces, no numbers, never evidence), labelled ILLUSTRATION.
  - `[TRAP REVEAL]`: the midpoint reveal.
- **Status:** draft 1, for the owner's approval. No video, frames, images or voice until the owner says "go".

**Timing (computed by `script_table.py`):** 130 rows · narration and trap waits 10:13 at ~2.6 words/s (music beds, act breaks and stings add ~30 s, so the cut runs about 10:43) · average hold 4.7 s · longest 6.9 s · pacing flags: none.
