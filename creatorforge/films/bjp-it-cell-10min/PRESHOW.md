## Facts
The full record is in `dossier.json`. It is based on the earlier 45-claim research file, with the key claims **checked live today**.

**Corrections the live check made:**
- **Twitter label:** Twitter's 2020 label was reported as the first such label **in India**, not the first on an Indian politician's post.
- **Kota, 2018:** reports differ on whether Shah added a caveat at Kota. His clearer "don't post fake news" instruction came at a Delhi meeting earlier in 2018. **Both are shown.**
- **Facebook 2019:** the 2.6 million follower figure is Alt News's, not Facebook's, so **it is not used**.
- **Google ad totals:** the reported figures conflict, so **only the consistent figures are used**.
- **The trends study:** it is shown with its own caveats. The campaigns were smaller than reported, and there was **no evidence anyone was paid**.

**Every allegation is followed by its response:**
- Decode, then the BJP's reply;
- the WSJ, then Facebook's reply;
- Chaturvedi, then the then IT head's denial;
- the Telangana arrests, then Congress's reply;
- the BJP's own case, given in its own terms.

**Red lines followed:**
- Tek Fog appears only as a retracted story, and the film says on screen that it is **not evidence**.
- The Amnesty abuse figures are not used.
- No ordinary account holders appear. The book's source is not named.
- The Sophie Zhang MP allegation is not used.
- Patra is identified as a spokesperson, not the IT cell.

**Neutral wording:** no loaded verbs.
- The closing line, "It changed drivers", is labelled on screen as **our reading**.
- No party colours are used, and no India outline map (states are shown as name tiles).
