# The BJP IT Cell: visual plan (draft storyboard)

Every shot is tied to a claim in `dossier.json` (**C##**). Each shot has a style tag: **V** is Vox (explain), **JH** is
Johnny Harris (investigate), **V+JH** is both. Each also has the engine line that will draw it. Figures are written in
only once the claim is checked against its original source. Badges mark status: REPORTED, ALLEGATION, PARTY CLAIM,
RECREATION.

**Look for this film:** dark charcoal paper, a red marker accent and typewriter labels, with a slow push on documents.
It is deliberately unlike the Aral Sea film's cream atlas look.

## Real material to collect (licensed or the parties' own)

- **The BJP's own upload of Amit Shah's Kota speech:** short excerpts with credit, for news reporting and comment.
- **The BJP's 2024 expenditure filing:** the Election Commission's PDF, shown as a document.
- **Platform records:** the Meta Ad Library, the Google Ads Transparency Center, Facebook's April 2019 takedown post.
- **Public figures' photos** (Bora, Malviya, Mhaskey, Swamy, Shah): licensed or Wikimedia, credited. No AI faces.
- **Archive rally photos:** 2014 and 2024, licensed.
- **Book cover of *I Am a Troll*:** shown small, credited, for review and comment.
- **Court and fact-check pages:** shown as documents.

## Never on screen

- Party symbols or flags used as decoration.
- Ordinary people's names, handles or faces: abuse is shown as blurred patterns.
- The Assam AI videos themselves (described, not shown).
- Tek Fog as evidence.
- A recreation that isn't labelled RECREATION.
