# The Infiltration (working title): script v2

- **Channel:** Dragon Psychology, long form, 16:9, target about 5 minutes.
- **Engine:** The Infiltration (owner's pick, 2026-11-20). A spy story: scientists go undercover in a 1954 UFO group, and the
  cover may have started to run the group. See `channels/PITCHES_2026-11-20_prophecy_remake.md`, column A.
- **Replaces:** `SCRIPT_TABLE.md` (draft 1, The Countdown). Not reused: the countdown clock, the lecture order, the evolution
  detour, the stick-figure house plan as the main stage, the study-card stacks.
- **Voice:** Kokoro `af_heart`, read at 1.1, lines joined into phrases. The owner hears the first 45 s before the full render
  (BRAIN_LOG 2026-11-20, rule 1).
- **Structure and voice:** the Vox × Johnny Harris five beats in the John Oliver voice: fact → receipt → absurd comparison →
  snap back. Written for motion (MASTER_DIRECTIVES §10, §10b, §10c). Viewer's stake at about 0:18 and again at the end. Kelly's first claim lands at about 2:10, the Riecken twist at about 2:30.
- **Palette:** slate #141824, lab-paper #F0F4F8, cobalt #2B6CB0 for structure, amber #ED8936 as the only signal colour. No
  yellow. Look: a case-file desk seen from above; paper cut-outs, real documents and photos, marker, highlighter, maps.
- **People:** no real faces, no AI likenesses, no presenter. Small markers appear only on the seating plan. The group's members
  are never mocked: they were ordinary people, deceived by researchers. Jokes target the study, the field, the methods and the
  aliens' scheduling. Dorothy Martin is named only as the dossier names her.
- **Allegations:** every claim against the researchers is attributed in the sentence ("according to Kelly…", "Kelly's paper
  says…"), and the other side gets its own beat: one scholar's reading, the authors have died and can't answer, no rebuttal found
  yet, his materials are public, other evidence on dissonance (including the 2024 multi-lab result).
- **Facts:** only from `dossier.json`. ⚠ marks an item to check before air.
- **Material:** real first. Real photos on disk are called by file name in backticks. `NEW REAL:` marks material to source.
  Reconstructions are labelled "RECONSTRUCTION". AI plates: none used (budget: at most three).
- **Bracket notes:** `[CUT]` = 0.2 s of silence before a punchline. `[HOLD 0.6s]` = a pause after a reveal.
- **Times** are estimates at about 2.65 spoken words per second plus pauses; re-time from the rendered voice.
