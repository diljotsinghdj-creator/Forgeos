# The Night the World Didn't End (working title): script, draft 1

- **Channel:** Dragon Psychology, long form, 16:9, about 11 minutes.
- **Engine:** A, *The Countdown*, with B's archive receipts in act 4 (owner's pick, 2026-11-19). Recorded in `pick.json`.
- **Structure and voice:** the Vox × Johnny Harris five beats, in the John Oliver voice: fact → receipt → simile → snap back.
  Written for motion (MASTER_DIRECTIVES §10b).
- **Canvas backbone:** the study desk: lab-paper sheets on slate. A cobalt clock face sits at the top of the desk all film long.
  **Clock 1** counts toward midnight, 20 Dec 1954. **Clock 2** starts in act 4 and reads "2025".
- **Palette:** slate #141824, lab-paper #F0F4F8, cobalt #2B6CB0 for structure, and amber #ED8936 as the only signal colour. No
  yellow.
- **People:** stick figures and black-and-white silhouettes only. No real faces are drawn, and no mocking of the group members.
  The satire targets the study, the field and the aliens' scheduling.
- **Allegations:** Kelly's archive findings are always attributed ("according to Kelly's paper…"), and the other side is shown.
- **Numbers and facts:** all are in `dossier.json`. ⚠ marks an item to check against the original before air.
- **Bracket notes:**
  - `[CUT]`: a 0.2 s music cut before a punchline, the only allowed hard cut.
  - `[HOLD 0.6s]`: a pause after a reveal.

## Guardrails this draft keeps
- **The group members are never mocked.** Their beliefs and their disappointment are told plainly; Martin's mental-health crisis
  and the children are not dramatised. Kelly's child-welfare allegation stays in the dossier, not the narration.
- **Every claim against the researchers is attributed to Kelly's paper** and his quoted archive notes. The other side gets its own
  beat: the authors can't respond, there's no rebuttal yet, his data is open, and dissonance is alive in other studies.
- **No real faces.** The researchers and the group are stick figures; documents are reconstructions labelled as such.
- **No John Oliver catchphrases.** The jokes aim at the study, the field and the aliens' timekeeping.
