## Numbers on screen
- **All numbers come from the dossier** (`data/dossier.json`). They are read by script from the World Bank API and NASA GISS files, or checked by a web search today.
- **Charts:** bars start at zero, and every chart says so.
- **Gig chart (056):** uses NITI Aayog's 2022 report. 7.7 m (2020–21) and 23.5 m (2029–30, labelled "projected"). This report was **not re-fetched today**.

## Changed after review (before you see it)
- **Wrong or unclear pictures:**
  - Lithium now shows a lithium cell, not AA alkaline.
  - A branded soda bottle is replaced.
  - Timbuktu no longer uses a photo of Djenné's mosque, which is in another city.
  - The Rift relief now lights Kenya, not "lowest ground". The low ground there is not the rift floor.
- **Satellite:**
  - The Barrier Reef, Malacca and the four map events now use sharper Sentinel-2 imagery.
- **Charts and text:**
  - The gig bars were made-up shapes. They are now NITI Aayog numbers.
  - The side-hustle bars are removed (no data).
  - Each clock now shows the time named on its frame.
  - Pearl Harbor's second wave reads "~8:54", because sources differ.

## Known weak spots
- **Dubai "islands" (083) and some before/after pairs** are soft. MODIS is 250 m per pixel, and no older sharp imagery is free.
- **A few cut-outs** went to torn-paper cards instead (rain cloud, bookshop, pharmacy, telegraph engraving, Hyderabad skyline).
- **Sand pile (180):** the simulated grains form a low mound rather than a steep cone.
