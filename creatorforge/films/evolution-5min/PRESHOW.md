## Techniques used
- **BiRefNet specimen cut-outs** of real fossils: Toumaï, P. boisei and the Neanderthal skull. The colour is kept and nothing is redrawn.
- **Photo-filled type:** "7,000,000" in fossil texture; "The crowd" in Haeckel's engraving.
- **A tilted 3D paper map** for the spread of H. erectus.
- **The log-time branch chart**, which grows through the film: species appear, the line at 110 ka crosses six branches, then the branches end one by one.
- **Brain-size circles** with area proportional to volume.
- **DNA strips, counters, stamps, highlighter sweeps and handwritten field notes.**
- **Event-driven sound design and the optical finish.**
