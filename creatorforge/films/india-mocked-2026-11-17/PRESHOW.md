- **No copyrighted material:** no news clips, no RRR footage or poster, and no Simpsons or Apu image.
- **The hateful captions are blacked out,** never shown.
- **India appears only in its official boundary.** The British India extent is simplified, and the frame says so.

- **Satire targets systems and documents:** "Author's qualifications: see line 2", "Moderation status: pending since 2019",
  "Definition updated without notice". There are no jokes about named people.
