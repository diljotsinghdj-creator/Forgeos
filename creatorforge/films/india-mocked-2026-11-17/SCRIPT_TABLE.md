# Disgust Pays (working title): script draft 3, written for motion

- **Format:** four-column, written for motion (printfacts-style, "Writing the script for motion"; MASTER_DIRECTIVES §10b).
- **Pick:** the owner's own draft (2026-11-17), corrected against `reports/Why India is mocked online.md` (`pick.json`).
- **Structure and voice:** the Vox × Johnny Harris five beats, in the John Oliver voice.
- **Canvas backbone:** a dark walnut investigator's desk. Every document, phone, map and photo slides onto it. The 3D globe, relief,
  bars and satellite open as "windows" on the desk and close back onto it.
- **Hard cuts:** only on comedy beats (marked `[CUT]`: 0.2 s music cut before a punchline, or a 1-frame smash).
- **Signal colour:** one per moment. Crimson #D93829 for evidence (circles, routes, stamps). Yellow #F7D046 only for highlighter
  sweeps.
- **Numbers:** all are in `dossier.json`. ⚠ marks a figure that rests on press reports, or a wording to check against the original.
- **Frame names** refer to the key frames already built. **Running time:** about 9 minutes.

## What changed from draft 2 (corrections and script-test pass, 2026-11-19)
The report's five corrections were already in draft 2: Macaulay instead of Kipling, no economy rank, the classifier gap instead of
"X bans slurs", receipts for the algorithm, and timing, not cause. This pass fixes what was left:
1. **The Macaulay line now matches his own words.** "A library he admits he never read" overstated the receipt. He wrote that he
   had no knowledge of Sanskrit or Arabic, so the line is now "by a reviewer who can't read the books".
2. **The 4chan origin is attributed to CNN.** The "2015" date had no source, so it is dropped.
3. **The visa fight is kept apart from the hostility.** The report asks for policy criticism of H-1B to be kept separate from
   ethnic hostility, so the line now says so on screen. The dense events row is split in two (pacing).
4. **The X payouts line** now says "engagement from Premium subscribers" (TechCrunch).
5. **The Kerala test account** now says the hate was mostly aimed at other Indians (domestic communal hate), as correction 3 asks.
   It is used as evidence of the language gap, not of hygiene videos.
6. **The pageant winners are replaced** by Sunita Williams. The pageant photos were never licensed, and the card now shows
   Chawla and Williams (NASA, public domain).
7. **"So. What. Now." is cut.** It played as a talk-show bit and a clunky transition (test 6, §10b), so it is replaced by a
   bridge on cause.
8. **The closing "since 1857" joke had no source** and is replaced by a payoff of the 1927 drain-inspector callback.

## How this draft meets the motion rules
- **Zoom-out hook:** it opens on one number in a real document (138.54M), then pulls out to the report, the phone and the stakes. The
  real report comes first, so gate 3 (evidence first) is met too.
- **Micro-loops:** each block runs artifact → anomaly (circled or highlighted) → curiosity gap (a question or "but…") → spatial pivot.
- **Cadence:** short snap-back lines (4–7 words) alternate with 15–20-word explanations.
- **Transitions:** no "Now let's look at", "Secondly" or "In conclusion". Sections bridge on cause.
- **One canvas:** everything lands on the same desk. The globe, bars, dial, relief and satellite open as windows and close back.
  Hard cuts happen only on `[CUT]` comedy beats.
- **Frame changes needed for the video:** some key frames now sit on their own backgrounds (charcoal or cream). At the video stage
  they'll be staged as sheets and windows on the desk, so the canvas stays continuous.
