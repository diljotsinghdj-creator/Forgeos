# PRESHOW: Panama, filled by rain (60 s sample, freehand)

**The film:** 56.9 s, 1920×1080, 24 fps, 13 shots, English.
**Narration:** Kokoro `bm_george` (en-GB), free and local.
**Score:** the studio's synthesised bed, ducked under the voice. Loudness is -15 LUFS.
**Rendered:** each shot is hand-composed in HTML/SVG, posed frame by frame in headless Chromium, and encoded with ffmpeg. No paid tool was used, and no image was AI-generated.
