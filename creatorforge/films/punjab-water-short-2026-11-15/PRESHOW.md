# Pre-show list: Punjab's Water Overdraft (Print Facts Short, video)

Read this before the video (gate 5).

## What it is
- **Format:** 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps, **73.5 s** (16 shots). The approved script ran about 58 s on paper; at a natural pace the narration is longer. It is still well within the YouTube Shorts limit.
- **Pacing (gate 4):** every shot moves (camera push plus build-ins), the longest shot is 6.6 s and the mean is 4.6 s, all within the limits.

## Choices made for you after "go"
I couldn't ask, so I went with my recommendations. Any of these can be swapped.
- **Beat 1:** RA, the five rivers flowing on the Sentinel-2 satellite view. The script's first line changed to *"This is Punjab, the land of five rivers. And its water account is in the red."*
- **Beat 2:** the bank statement card, then the in/out columns.
- **Beat 5:** 5H, the paper map with the four study areas, then 5C, the 4.8 km cube of water.
- **Long beats split:**
  - beat 7: a new "free power since 1997" card, then the chain;
  - beat 9: the trend line, then a new canal shot ("canals take some of the load").

## The voice
- **Stand-in:** Kokoro **am_onyx**, a stand-in, **not your ElevenLabs voice**. It was chosen so it doesn't repeat the last piece's voice (bf_alice).
- **Pronunciation:** Kokoro may pronounce Indian place names imperfectly; your voice would fix that.

## Sound
- **Music:** a quiet pad and pulse, synthesised in the studio (no licence needed). It ducks under the voice.
- **Sound effects:** the studio's built-in set (whoosh, impact, pop, pen, paper).
- **Loudness:** −14 LUFS.

## Captions
- **Style:** word by word, with the current word in signature yellow.
- **Timing:** estimated from word length, because there is no forced alignment on this machine. A word can lead or lag by a fraction of a second.

## Numbers
All numbers are in `dossier.json`.
- **Still unchecked against the PDFs:** the government sites (CGWB, Parliament, PIB) refuse connections from this machine. Figures are confirmed from search results that show those pages.
- **Labelled on screen:**
  - the GRACE figure covers north-west India;
  - ~3,000 L per kilo is an estimate with a range;
  - ~0.5 m a year is from data up to 2017;
  - "desert in 25 years" is from a 2019 **draft** report.
- **Rivers:** only three of the five rivers flow through Indian Punjab. The map shows the whole Punjab region with India's official boundary.
