## Numbers and how sure they are
- **Series:** every number is from the 2025 national groundwater assessment (Ministry of Jal Shakti / CGWB) unless the frame says otherwise:
  - 156.36%;
  - 16.80 and 26.27 BCM;
  - 111 of 153 blocks;
  - Rajasthan 147% and Haryana 137%.
- **Not read from the official PDFs:** the government sites refuse connections from this machine. Every figure was matched against search results showing those official pages ([ACCESS / SYSTEM BLOCKER]). Allowing those sites lets me check the PDFs before the video.
- **Labelled on the frames:**
  - the GRACE loss is for north-west India, not Punjab alone (Nature, 2009);
  - "~3,000 L per kg" is the ICAR estimate, and the frame gives the range;
  - "~0.5 m a year" is dated to data up to 2017;
  - "desert in 25 years" is a 2019 **draft** report, as the press reported it.
