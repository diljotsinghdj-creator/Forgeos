## 12. What we can adapt (original equivalents, never copies)
- [O] **"This fear is older than you think":** open a present-day anxiety, then cut to a dated primary document (a newspaper page, a speech transcript, a patent) showing the same fear decades ago. Fits Print Facts' evidence-first gate.
- [O] **The aged prediction:** find a sourced expert forecast that turned out wrong (with date and citation) and stage it as a card that later gets struck through in #FFDD33.
- [O] **Baseline line chart with shaded exceptions:** draw a long series, shade the shocks (recessions, wars), and let the viewer see the line return; the argument is the return.
- [O] **Counter-signal beat:** after the main claim, show one indicator that should move if the scary story were true and show that it did not (or did). One chart, one sentence.
- [O] **End on the unsettled variable:** close on the question the data cannot settle yet (who captures the gains), stated plainly, rather than a verdict.
