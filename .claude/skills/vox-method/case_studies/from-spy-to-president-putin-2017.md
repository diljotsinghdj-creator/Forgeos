## 1. Subject and central question
- [V] The description frames Putin's rise from intelligence officer to president and his push against the Western order; it highlights his shaping of Russia into an authoritarian, militaristic society, his view of the Soviet collapse as a loss of territory, his government's cyber operations, and says Donald Trump's election offered new hope for his aims (Trakt description).
- [I] Central question: "what does Putin want, and where did that come from?" A biography used as a key to a foreign policy, published in March 2017 when the 2016 election interference story was at its peak.

## 2. Opening technique
- [V] Cold open on the present, not the spy past: the narration sums up Putin's rule since 1999 (authoritarian, militaristic, two neighbours invaded, ties with Syria and Iran, pushing back on the Western order), followed by chanting and a short archival clip calling him the most powerful man in the world (transcript, after a "[Music]" tag).
- [V] Only then does it go back to the Soviet collapse, with Putin as a 40-year-old KGB officer in East Germany when the Berlin Wall fell (transcript).

## 3. Narrative structure
- [V] Chapter list (rosetta.to, YouTube-style): untitled 0:00 → "Was Putin mayor of St Petersburg?" 1:42 → "What did Putin do to Crimea?" 7:58.
