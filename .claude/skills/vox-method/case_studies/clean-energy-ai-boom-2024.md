- [O] **Answer a real viewer question:** open on a real, consented comment or letter (text on screen, no face), and promise a direct answer by the end. Keep consent on file.
- [O] **Household equivalence, sourced:** translate an abstract quantity into a count of ordinary units (homes, printing presses, books per day), with the conversion shown in the dossier. Double-check the number: this case shows how easily such figures get garbled.
