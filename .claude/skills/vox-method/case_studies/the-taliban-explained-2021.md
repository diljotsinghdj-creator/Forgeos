## 12. What we can adapt (original equivalents, never copies)
- [O] **Return loop.** Open on the latest event, flash back to origins, and close by returning to the same opening frame with the viewer now able to read it. The repeated frame changes meaning, not content (our gate 2 bars repeating it across films, not within one).
- [O] **Second-time question.** For anything that happened twice (a market crash, a species' return, a law repealed and re-passed), structure the film around "what was different the second time?" with a two-column then/now comparison, differences highlighted in yellow #FFDD33.
- [O] **Explain after, not during.** Schedule topical Print Facts films weeks after the news peak, when documents are available, so the evidence-first gate can be met.
- [O] **Classroom-ready packaging.** State the runtime and give a short chapter list in the description; teachers and fact-checkers cite what they can navigate.
