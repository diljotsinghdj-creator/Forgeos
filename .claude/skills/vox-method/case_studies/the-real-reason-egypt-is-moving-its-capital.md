Evidence labels used below:

## 1. Subject and central question
- [V] Egypt's government is building a new administrative capital roughly 40-45 km east of Cairo. The official reason is Cairo's overcrowding; the film argues the real aim is political: a seat of power far from the crowds that filled Tahrir Square in 2011 (summaries).
- [V] Storybench says the story covered the capital move and the abandoned or half-empty desert cities around Cairo.

## 2. Opening technique
- Not verified.
- [I] The title format "The real reason X" sets up official-story vs real-story; the opening likely states the official reason first so it can be tested. Inferred from title and summaries.

## 3. Narrative structure
- [V] Official reason (overcrowding) → history of Egypt building desert cities to decongest Cairo, starting with Nasr City in the 1950s → who the new housing is for (mostly middle and upper class) → political reading (distance from protest) (summaries).
- [I] Structure is a hypothesis test: state the claim, check it against history and against who actually benefits, then offer the alternative.

## 4. Narration characteristics
- [V] Atlas is a map-first series explaining international stories (Ellis via Storybench).
- [I] Argument is made by geography (distance, location, who lives where) more than by quotes.

## 12. What we can adapt (original equivalents, never copies)
- [O] **Official vs measured.** State the official reason on screen as a quote card with source; then measure it with one map variable (distance, elevation, who lives within X km) and show the result.
- [O] **Precedent row.** Before claiming a motive, show earlier attempts of the same kind and how they turned out; a row of past cases is evidence, the motive is interpretation (label it).
- [O] **Master map.** Build one master map per film and derive every zoom from it, so scale jumps are continuous and the viewer never loses where they are.
- [O] **Who benefits filter.** For any infrastructure story, add a beat that shows who can afford or reach the new thing, with sourced figures.
