## Measured patterns

1. **The question lands around a minute in, after a concrete cold open,** not in the first 20 seconds. Measured:
   0:27 (desserts, watch analysis), ~0:45–1:00 (Paris, El Niño, prices), 1:10 (forest), 1:40 (EV), ~2:05
   (birthright, after a news montage). Median **about 1:00** [V]. Our earlier guidance said 0:20–0:40; it is
   corrected in vox_storytelling_methodology.md.
2. **Narration speed is about 167 words a minute** (147–190 across eight sampled segments, about 2.8 words a
   second) [V]. Our timing estimate is corrected from 2.6 to 2.8 words a second.
3. **Sentences in the punctuated transcripts run a median of 15–16 words** (range 7–35) [V]: longer than our earlier
   10–14 guidance, with short punch sentences mixed in.
4. **Arguments by causes are signposted aloud** ("the first reason", "the second way", "the last big reason") in
   three of six [V].
5. **The ending returns to the opening** (the refrain, the company, the chant, the sharks) in five of six [V].
6. **Sound as evidence:** the field recording is played to the viewer to show the problem (animals, 2:22) [V].
7. **A returning chart** is used as a deliberate anchor (prices: the same inflation chart three times) [V].
8. **Sponsor or membership reads sit in the middle third** (2:55, 3:03, 4:12, 7:16 across four videos) [V], or at
   the very end.
9. **Expert voices enter as soundbites,** not as long interviews (EV, animals, prices) [V]. Faceless adaptation:
   quote cards with the source, or the expert's own document.
