# Channel sample: Vox's 270 most recent uploads (read 2026-11-12)

**Source:** the public upload list of youtube.com/@Vox, read on 2026-11-12. For each upload it gives the title, the
length and the rounded view count. These are **[V] direct observations of packaging**: titles, lengths, series labels
and views. Transcripts and frames were **not** accessed: YouTube refused them to this session, and we don't get round
its bot checks.

**Caveats:**
- View counts depend on age, topic and the news. Read the patterns below as tendencies, not causes.
- The list mixes explainers with podcast videos (The Gray Area, Today Explained, America Actually).
- Partner (sponsored) content is excluded from the standalone stats.

## What we take from it [O]

- Pitch titles in the "Why…" / "How…" / question forms first, around 30–50 characters, with a concrete noun.
- Plan explainers at 8–14 minutes. A film running over 15 minutes needs a reason.
- The deictic "this" title needs a thumbnail whose object is instantly recognisable.
- Re-read this sample every few months (learning_protocol.md) to track drift in format and length.
