# The surge in online hate against Indians and people of Indian origin (2023–2026)

Research notes compiled 2026-11-17 for a neutral documentary (Print Facts / Dragon Psychology research dossier).

**How these notes were made, and their limits (read first):**
- Every page fetch failed in this session because the network blocks www.csohate.org, www.newsgram.com and similar hosts. **All findings below come from search-engine summaries of the pages and press coverage. No primary report was read in full.** Before any figure goes on screen, open the primary PDF or page and check it. Then change the status to "confirmed (primary checked)".
- **Status key:**
  - **confirmed:** official statistics, or the same fact appears in several independent outlets;
  - **reported:** a single monitoring group or outlet says it, or the claim was not cross-checked;
  - **contested:** the sources disagree;
  - **opinion:** commentary or advocacy.
- **Monitoring-group caveats that apply to every count below:**
  - each group (CSOH, NCRI, Moonshot/Stop AAPI Hate, ISD) uses its own keyword lists, platforms, time windows and engagement thresholds, so their numbers **cannot be added or compared directly**;
  - "views" is X's impression metric and is not unique people;
  - keyword counts include some quoting, reclaiming and counter-speech unless they are filtered out;
  - several groups have their own positions: CSOH focuses on Hindu-nationalist as well as anti-Indian hate; NCRI has been criticised by some academics for publishing outside peer review; COHNA and HinduACT are Hindu advocacy groups.
  - None of these reports is peer-reviewed.

## 1. What monitoring groups measure (numbers, slurs, narratives, platforms, dates)

### Takeaway
Four separate monitoring efforts all report a sharp rise in anti-Indian and anti-South Asian content online from late 2024 into 2026. The volume is concentrated on X, and the peaks line up with immigration-policy news (H-1B, tariffs, visa fees) and with US politics. The headline figures run from about 128 viral posts (CSOH, Jan 2025) to about 542,000 posts in 18 months (CSOH, Sep 2026). The methods differ a lot.

### Cited Findings

**Center for the Study of Organized Hate (CSOH)**
- **Report 1, Jan 9 2025:** "Anti-Indian Hate on X: How the Platform Amplifies Racism and Xenophobia."
  - It documented **128 X posts** that targeted Indians, tied to the Trump transition's appointment of Sriram Krishnan as AI policy adviser and to a Vivek Ramaswamy post.
  - The posts had **138.54 million views as of Jan 3 2025**.
  - **125 of the 128 posts were still live** when the report came out.
  - Status: reported. Sources: [CSOH, 9 Jan 2025](https://www.csohate.org/2025/01/09/x-anti-indian-racism/); [CSOH press release](https://www.csohate.org/press-releases/new-report-surge-in-anti-indian-racism-and-xenophobia-on-x-after-krishnan-appointment-ramaswamy-tweet/).
- **Report 2, Sep 16 2025:** "Anti-Indian Racism on X (July–September 2025)."
  - It covered **680 high-engagement posts** with **281.2 million views** between **1 Jul and 7 Sep 2025**.
  - **121 posts (17.8%) used anti-Indian slurs** and drew **74.3 million views**.
  - **August had nearly five times the anti-Indian hate of July**, which CSOH links to the start of US–India tariff tensions.
  - Immigration and expulsion themes made up **nearly 70%** of the dataset. The main drivers were the H-1B visa and resentment over STEM jobs, with Indians framed as "job stealers."
  - Status: reported. Sources: [CSOH, 16 Sep 2025](https://www.csohate.org/2025/09/16/anti-indian-racism-on-x-july-september-2025/); [CSOH on X](https://x.com/csohate/status/1968344193602101289); [American Bazaar, 18 Sep 2025](https://americanbazaaronline.com/2025/09/18/anti-indian-racism-on-x-on-the-rise-report-467757/).
- **Report 3, Sep 28 2026:** "The Rising Tide of Anti-South Asian Hate in the United States."
  - It covers **X, 4chan, 8chan and Gab** from **1 Jan 2025 to 30 Jun 2026**.
  - It counted about **542,360 anti-South Asian hate posts on X** over the 18 months.
  - Monthly posts on X rose **240%**, from **12,904 in Jan 2024 to 43,843 in Jun 2026**. The press release headline says "240% from January 2024 to June 2026."
  - Between Jan 2025 and Jun 2026 alone, monthly volume rose **75%**.
  - **Five of the six highest months fell between Nov 2025 and Jun 2026.**
  - About **45%** of posts framed South Asians as "invaders", "job thieves" or a demographic threat.
  - The slur **"pajeet" appeared in nearly 30%** of anti-South Asian posts on X.
  - The **fastest-growing category was hygiene-based dehumanisation, up 171%**.
  - On the fringe platforms, slurs were the most common form of hate. One summary (SCMP) says the slur appeared in 45% of the fringe-platform mentions.
  - CSOH names these drivers: immigration policy and political rhetoric, economic resentment, the growing political visibility of South Asians, amplification by platforms, and **generative AI used to make racist caricatures**.
  - Status: reported. The 240% baseline (Jan 2024) falls outside the stated study window (from Jan 2025). This inconsistency needs checking in the primary report. Sources: [CSOH report, 28 Sep 2026](https://www.csohate.org/2026/09/28/anti-south-asian-hate-united-states-2026/); [CSOH press release](https://www.csohate.org/press-releases/anti-south-asian-hate-united-states-2026/); [Al Jazeera, 28 Sep 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/28/anti-south-asian-hate-speech-has-exploded-online-in-us-new-report-finds); [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/week-asia/politics/article/3369473/anti-south-asian-online-hate-surges-us-under-trump-20-study-finds); [American Bazaar, 28 Sep 2026](https://americanbazaaronline.com/2026/09/28/anti-south-asian-hate-rises-across-us-online-platforms-with-over-500k-hate-posts-on-x-488951/).
- A social post by India First Post said "2025 posts up 60%." **This is not verified against CSOH and should not be used.** Source: [X post](https://x.com/ifpost47/status/2104987832637120837).

**Network Contagion Research Institute (NCRI)**
- **Report, published about Mar 2026:** "From Policy Drift to Purity Grift: How a Small Network Hijacked the Immigration Debate."
  - It tracked **more than 24,000 anti-Indian posts on X from Jan to Dec 2025**, with **over 300 million views**.
  - Weekly volume **roughly tripled** compared with 2024.
  - The peak came in **mid-December 2025**, with **more than 800 posts a week using slurs such as "pajeet" and "dothead"**.
  - Spikes lined up with policy events: the **DHS H-1B modernisation rule**, **State Department visa restrictions** and the **proposed US$100,000 H-1B petition fee**.
  - **The rhetoric changed:** early in 2025 explicit slurs dominated. From mid-2025 the posts moved to "policy-framed" hostility, claiming Indian workers were "replacing" Americans. NCRI suggests this may be a way to avoid moderation (this is NCRI's interpretation).
  - **The activity was concentrated:** NCRI says three prolific accounts drew more than 10% of likes and about 20% of reposts. It names Nick Fuentes and Sneako as amplifiers. NCRI argues the surge was driven by a small network rather than spreading on its own.
  - Status: reported. Sources: [NCRI report page](https://networkcontagion.us/reports/from-policy-drift-to-purity-grift-how-a-small-network-hijacked-the-immigration-debate/); [Business Standard, 12 Mar 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/immigration/indians-in-us-called-invaders-scammers-hate-posts-on-x-drew-300-mn-views-126031200623_1.html); [Newsgram, 12 Mar 2026](https://www.newsgram.com/indian-diaspora/2026/03/12/ncri-study-anti-india-rhetoric-x-2025); [American Bazaar, 12 Mar 2026](https://americanbazaaronline.com/2026/03/12/how-2025-became-a-minefield-for-indian-americans-476724/).
- **NCRI 2022:** a study from NCRI's Rutgers period reportedly named "pajeet" as a primary marker of online Indophobia and anti-Hindu memes. Status: reported, through a weak secondary source. Source: [Bharatpedia](https://en.bharatpedia.org/wiki/Pajeet). **Verify on networkcontagion.us.**
- COHNA, a Hindu advocacy group, presents the NCRI research as showing that **Hindu traditions, festivals and temples** were targeted. Status: opinion/advocacy framing. Sources: [India Currents](https://indiacurrents.com/hindu-advocacy-day-on-capitol-hill-celebrates-america-250/); [COHNA](https://cohna.org/hindu-advocacy-day-capitol-hill-america-250-bipartisan-support-from-congressional-reps-rising-hinduphobia-and-hate/).

**Moonshot data, published by Stop AAPI Hate**
- **Dec 2024 to Jan 2025:** **73% of anti-Asian slurs and 75% of anti-Asian threats** in the extremist online spaces Moonshot monitors were aimed at **South Asians**. Anti-South Asian slurs rose **75% in January compared with November**. The data covers forums including Reddit, X and 4chan. Status: reported. Sources: [Stop AAPI Hate, 20 Feb 2025](https://stopaapihate.org/2025/02/20/post-election-surge-in-hate/); [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/asian-america/anti-south-asian-online-hate-surged-rcna193006).
- **Summer 2025:** compared with a 12-month baseline, **"rag head" rose 192%** and **"towelhead" rose 175%**. Stop AAPI Hate links the rise partly to **Zohran Mamdani's NYC mayoral campaign**. It calls **"pajeet" the most commonly used anti-Asian slur in extremist online spaces**. Status: reported. Source: [Stop AAPI Hate, 7 Aug 2025](https://stopaapihate.org/2025/08/07/keeping-count-behind-the-spike-in-anti-south-asian-hate/).
- **Nov 2025:** the surge "continued to escalate through July and August and remained high in September." Status: reported. Sources: [Stop AAPI Hate, 4 Nov 2025](https://stopaapihate.org/2025/11/04/keeping-count-anti-south-asian-hate-its-gotten-worse/); [Stop AAPI Hate, 7 Nov 2025 (Mamdani)](https://stopaapihate.org/2025/11/07/keeping-count-mamdani-hate-nyc-mayoral-race/).
- **"The State of Anti-South Asian Hate" (2026):**
  - Anti-South Asian slurs in online spaces tied to targeted violence rose from **22,996 (Jan 2023) to 48,033 (Dec 2025), up 109%**.
  - A national survey found that **48% of South Asian adults said they experienced a hate act in 2025** because of race, ethnicity or nationality.
  - Status: reported. The publication date is unclear (one listing says Sep 2026). Source: [India West](https://indiawest.com/anti-south-asian-slurs-surge-109-in-us/).

**Institute for Strategic Dialogue (ISD)**
- **Canada report, about June 2025:** posts on X with **anti-South Asian slurs rose more than 1,350% from 2023 to 2024**. The hostility was often framed around **housing and job worries** and aimed largely at Indians amid the **Khalistan / India–Canada tensions**. Status: reported. Source: [ISD Digital Dispatch](https://www.isdglobal.org/digital-dispatch/the-rise-of-anti-south-asian-hate-in-canada/).
- I found **no ISD report on the US H-1B episode**. The July–Sep 2025 dataset that some outlets credit loosely belongs to **CSOH**, not ISD.

**Mainstream coverage**
- **CNN, 16 Nov 2025:** "Indian Americans are being openly targeted in America." It reports that slurs, some starting on 4chan, are entering everyday language online and offline. Status: reported. Source: [CNN](https://edition.cnn.com/2025/11/16/us/indian-americans-racism-maga-cec).

### Inferences
- Four groups with different methods agree on the **direction**: a sharp rise from late 2024 to 2026, peaking around policy news. That agreement is stronger evidence than any single number. The **size** of the rise depends heavily on method (1,350%, 240%, 109%, "tripled").
- The CSOH and NCRI findings are consistent with each other: the activity is concentrated in a small number of high-reach accounts, and posts moved from crude slurs to "policy" framing. For the film this is useful: the "economic argument" can carry the same hostility.
- Moonshot's figure that 73–75% of anti-Asian slurs and threats targeted South Asians suggests the focus of anti-Asian online hate moved away from East Asians, the main target during COVID, toward South Asians in 2024–25. This is an inference. Check whether Stop AAPI Hate says so explicitly.

### Gaps
- **ADL, CCDH and Moonshot standalone reports:** none were found on anti-Indian hate specifically. ADL's general audits may include South Asian data. This was not searched successfully.
- **Primary methodology:** no primary methodology for CSOH, NCRI or Moonshot was read (fetches were blocked). Keyword lists, sampling and the definition of "hate post" are unknown.
- **Platform coverage:** there is no platform-level data for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or Reddit beyond Moonshot's forum mix.
- **Baseline:** there is no measure of anti-Indian content as a **share** of all X posts. If total X volume or the bot share changed, raw counts can mislead.

## 2. Triggers and spikes

### Takeaway
The spikes follow news: the H-1B fight on X in December 2024, the US–India tariff dispute in August 2025, US visa-policy moves in 2025 (including the US$100k H-1B fee), Mamdani's NYC campaign in 2025, and local events in Canada, Ireland, Australia and the UK. A 2010 political-science finding (Hopkins) predicts exactly this pattern: hostility rises when national rhetoric frames a visible group as a threat.

### Cited Findings

**US: the H-1B fight on X, December 2024**
- **How it started:** Laura Loomer attacked Trump's pick of **Sriram Krishnan** as senior White House AI policy adviser over the Christmas holiday. She cited Krishnan's earlier support for removing country caps on green cards. Status: confirmed (several outlets). Sources: [The Hill](https://thehill.com/business/5060579-h-1b-visas-musk-maga-trump-ramaswamy-bannon-loomer-krishnan-immigration/); [Daily Caller, 29 Dec 2024](https://dailycaller.com/2024/12/29/trump-silicon-valley-sriram-krishnan-maga-immigration-elon-musk-vivek-ramaswamy-doge-h1b/).
- **Ramaswamy's post, 26 Dec 2024:** he defended tech's reliance on foreign talent and said American culture "venerated mediocrity over excellence." It drew strong pushback. **Elon Musk defended H-1B**, and **Trump then said he shared Musk's view** ("It's a great program"). Status: confirmed. Sources: [CNBC, 27 Dec 2024](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/12/27/elon-musk-and-vivek-ramaswamy-open-rift-with-maga-loyalists-over-skilled-worker-visas.html); [ABC News](https://abcnews.go.com/Politics/musk-ramaswamy-spar-trump-supporters-support-1b-work/story?id=117147209); [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/trump-musk-ramaswamy-immigration-h1b-visa-workers-2006669).
- **How much of the backlash was racist (contested):** CNBC described the first reaction as a "racially charged backlash." Another outlet said only a small fringe made racist remarks and most objections were about Krishnan's immigration views. Status: **contested**. Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/12/27/elon-musk-and-vivek-ramaswamy-open-rift-with-maga-loyalists-over-skilled-worker-visas.html); [YourDestinationNow](https://www.yourdestinationnow.com/2024/12/trump-backs-musk-on-immigration-visas.html) (a low-authority source). CSOH's count of 128 posts with 138.5M views (Section 1) is the measured side.
- **Counter-view for the film:** H-1B critics include labour advocates and immigration restrictionists across the political spectrum. They argue on wages and outsourcing, not race. The film should keep **policy criticism of H-1B** separate from **ethnic hostility**. NCRI's finding that slurs moved into "policy framing" shows why the two are hard to tell apart.

**US: 2025 events**
- **US–India tariff tensions, August 2025:** CSOH links the near-fivefold jump from July to August to the start of these tensions. Status: reported (CSOH's interpretation). Source: [CSOH on X](https://x.com/csohate/status/1968344193602101289).
- **Visa-policy moves:** the DHS H-1B modernisation rule, State Department visa restrictions and the proposed **US$100,000 H-1B petition fee** each lined up with spikes in slurs and deportation rhetoric. Status: reported (NCRI). Source: [NCRI](https://networkcontagion.us/reports/from-policy-drift-to-purity-grift-how-a-small-network-hijacked-the-immigration-debate/).
- **Zohran Mamdani's NYC mayoral campaign (2025)** is linked to a summer 2025 spike in anti-South Asian slurs. Status: reported (Stop AAPI Hate). Sources: [Stop AAPI Hate, Aug 2025](https://stopaapihate.org/2025/08/07/keeping-count-behind-the-spike-in-anti-south-asian-hate/); [Stop AAPI Hate, Nov 2025](https://stopaapihate.org/2025/11/07/keeping-count-mamdani-hate-nyc-mayoral-race/).
- **Dallas killing, 10 Sep 2025:**
  - Indian-origin motel manager **Chandra Nagamallaiah** was killed with a machete. A co-worker, **Yordanis Cobos-Martinez (37)**, a Cuban national, was charged with capital murder. ICE said he had been released because Cuba would not accept him back. The CCTV footage spread widely.
  - **Trump** condemned the killing and used it to attack "Illegal Immigrant Criminals." The fundraiser for the family raised more than US$257,000.
  - **Investigators have not identified a racial or religious motive.** The trigger was reportedly a dispute over a washing machine. A COHNA Canada opinion piece alleges the accused made Hinduphobic remarks and says the media ignored the case.
  - Status: the killing and the charge are confirmed; the motive is **not established**; the COHNA claims are opinion.
  - Sources: [Deseret News, 19 Sep 2025](https://www.deseret.com/politics/2025/09/19/chandra-nagamallaiah-murder-trump-made-a-comment-on-illegal-immigration); [CBS Texas](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/texas/news/dallas-motel-manager-remembered-kindness-machete-killing); [Outlook India](https://www.outlookindia.com/international/texas-murder-indian-origin-motel-manager-beheaded-in-dallas-co-worker-charged); [American Kahani (opinion)](https://americankahani.com/perspectives/why-a-brutal-beheading-of-a-brown-man-was-met-with-media-silence-while-brutal-stabbing-of-a-blonde-woman-became-a-cause-celebre/).
  - **Note for the film:** this is **not** an example of anti-Indian hate crime. It shows how a killing becomes material for a separate immigration narrative.
- **CNN, Nov 2025:** reports open targeting of Indian Americans in MAGA-aligned online spaces. Status: reported. Source: [CNN](https://edition.cnn.com/2025/11/16/us/indian-americans-racism-maga-cec).

**Canada: immigration levels, housing, international students, and the 2023 diplomatic row**
- **The India–Canada row:** it followed the killing of Khalistan activist Hardeep Singh Nijjar. Canada alleged Indian government involvement; **India denies it**. Study permits issued to Indians then fell from **108,940 (Q3 2023) to 14,910 (Q4 2023), down 86%**. Reuters-based reporting puts part of the fall down to India expelling the Canadian diplomats who processed permits, and part to fewer applications. Status: the permit numbers are reported (Reuters via Tribune/Gulf News, citing IRCC data); the allegation over the killing is contested between the two governments. Sources: [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/after-hardeep-singh-nijjar-row-86-fall-in-pupils-headed-to-canada-582165); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/americas/canada-sees-big-drop-in-study-permits-issued-to-indian-students-1.100482159).
- **The 2024 study-permit cap:**
  - Canada capped study permits, at about **292,000** according to one outlet. Officials linked the cap partly to the **housing shortage**. Immigration Minister **Marc Miller** called the system "out of control." He also said a cap was not a "one-size-fits-all solution" to housing.
  - The Indian High Commission offered another explanation: poor housing and teaching facilities at some Canadian institutions.
  - Status: the cap is confirmed; the 292k figure is reported (single outlet, check against IRCC).
  - Sources: [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/canada-caps-study-permits-for-international-students-at-292000); [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/9914125/ns-advocates-international-student-cap-housing-crunch); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/americas/canada-sees-big-drop-in-study-permits-issued-to-indian-students-1.100482159).
- **Online framing:** ISD found anti-South Asian hate in Canada framed around "limited housing and employment anxieties." Slur posts on X rose more than 1,350% from 2023 to 2024. Status: reported. Source: [ISD](https://www.isdglobal.org/digital-dispatch/the-rise-of-anti-south-asian-hate-in-canada/).

**Ireland: July–August 2025 assaults**

  - An Indian man in his 40s was assaulted at Parkhill Lawns, Kilnamanagh, Dublin 24, and left stripped and bleeding. **Gardaí recorded it as racially motivated** and said he was "wrongly targeted." The attack followed a false online accusation against him (see the Irish Times account).
  - Gardaí arrested a man in his 30s and a male juvenile.
  - Some outlets give 26 Jul. That is the Irish Times publication date, not the date of the attack.
  - Status: confirmed (Garda statement reported by several outlets).
  - Sources: [Irish Times, 26 Jul 2025](https://www.irishtimes.com/crime-law/2025/07/26/he-kept-saying-what-wrong-have-i-done-why-me-an-indian-man-is-left-stripped-and-bloodied-on-an-irish-street); [Dublin People, 21 Jul 2025](https://dublinpeople.com/news/southside/articles/2025/07/21/crowe-criticises-racist-attack-in-tallaght/); [The Week/PTI, 22 Jul 2025](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/international/2025/07/22/fgn53-ireland-indian-attack.html).
  - **Check the Irish Times piece for the false-accusation detail before using it.** The search summary did not confirm it.
- **Wider pattern and responses:**
  - The **Ireland India Council** chair said he was receiving reports of **one or two attacks a day**. The council believed the same group might be behind four other attacks. Status: reported (community group's claim).
  - The **Indian Embassy issued a safety advisory on 1 Aug 2025** about more physical attacks on Indian citizens. More than 100 people from the Indian community held a silent protest at the Department of Justice on 25 Jul. Justice Minister **Jim O'Callaghan** said racist attacks "won't be tolerated." Status: confirmed.
  - Sources: [Echo Live / Irish Examiner group](https://www.echolive.ie/nationalnews/arid-41680493.html); [TheJournal.ie, Aug 2025](https://www.thejournal.ie/indian-embassy-in-ireland-issues-safety-advisory-after-recent-attacks-6780443-Aug2025/); [Dublin People, 20 Aug 2025](https://dublinpeople.com/news/southside/articles/2025/08/20/ocallaghan-indian-attacks/); [Business Today, 8 Aug 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/nri/story/attack-on-indians-in-ireland-why-are-migrants-facing-rising-tide-of-racial-violence-488447-2025-08-08); [Times Higher Education](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/south-asian-students-constant-fear-amid-racist-attacks).

**Australia: "March for Australia", 31 Aug 2025**
- **Flyers and the rallies:** organisers' flyers singled out Indian migration ("More Indians in five years than Greeks and Italians in 100"). Rallies were held nationwide. Police put protesters and counter-protesters together at **more than 50,000**; another estimate gives about 52,400 protesters, with up to 8,000 in Sydney. Status: the flyer is reported by several outlets; **the crowd numbers are contested and are estimates**.

  - In Melbourne, police used pepper spray during clashes.
  - A man who identified as Indian was shoved while speaking at a rally.
  - The Age reported that neo-Nazi **Thomas Sewell** spoke in Melbourne. **Pauline Hanson** attended the Canberra event.
  - Indian community leaders advised people to stay indoors.

- Status: confirmed for the clashes and the government condemnation.

  - A second racially aggravated rape of a woman in her 20s of Punjabi Sikh heritage was reported. Police said at the time that they were "not linking this attack to any other offences." A man was arrested.
  - Status: confirmed.
  - Sources: [Express & Star, 26 Oct 2025](https://www.expressandstar.com/uk-news/2025/10/26/rape-of-woman-in-walsall-being-treated-as-racially-aggravated-police-say); [Express & Star, 27 Oct 2025](https://www.expressandstar.com/news/2025/10/27/deeply-shocked-and-saddened-birmingham-mp-speaks-out-against-recent-racially-aggrevated-attacks-in-the-black-country); [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/diaspora/napa-condemns-racially-aggravated-rape-of-sikh-woman-in-uks-walsall).
- **Harrow, May 2025:** three Hindu-Indian teenagers were reportedly attacked after being asked about their ethnicity. Status: reported by a Hindu advocacy outlet only. Source: [INSIGHT UK](https://insightuk.org/is-london-a-hotbed-for-anti-hindu-and-anti-indian-hate-crime/).
- **UK counter-current and contested framing:**
  - Some UK coverage concerns **conflict between communities**, such as the Hindu–Muslim unrest in Leicester in 2022 and claims of Islamist hostility toward Hindus (Organiser, a partisan RSS-linked outlet). It also covers a leaked NPCC review on "Hindu nationalist extremism," which Hindu groups dispute.
  - This is a different phenomenon from white-nationalist anti-Indian hate. **Keep it separate or attribute it carefully.**
  - Status: contested and opinion.
  - Sources: [Spiked, 29 Apr 2025](https://www.spiked-online.com/2025/04/29/does-britain-really-have-a-hindu-extremism-problem/); [Organiser, 12 Mar 2026](https://organiser.org/2026/03/12/343770/world/uk-rising-islamist-hostility-hindu-festival-disrupted-shops-of-indians-vandalised-at-wembley/).

### Inferences
- There is a recurring pattern: (1) a policy or news event puts Indians in the frame (visa, tariff, a visible politician or appointee, a migration-number flyer); (2) a small set of high-reach accounts frames it; (3) volume spikes; (4) in some countries (Ireland, Australia, the UK), offline incidents follow. **The film can show the correlation in time, not cause.** No source establishes that online posts caused any specific offline attack.
- The pattern fits Hopkins's "politicized places" model (see Section 4). Hostility appears where a group's local visibility has grown quickly **and** national rhetoric frames it as a threat.

### Gaps
- There is no rigorous study linking online spikes to offline incidents. Avoid any statement that X posts "caused" an attack.
- **Ireland:** no court outcome for the Tallaght case was found. No Garda statistics on anti-Indian incidents in 2025 were found.
- **Australia:** there are no official hate-crime statistics for Indians. The Scanlon Foundation "Mapping Social Cohesion" survey, which measures discrimination by birthplace, was not reached.
- **US:** the CSOH/NCRI attribution of the August 2025 tariff spike is their own interpretation. No independent time-series check was found.
- The 2023 Khalistan dispute's direct effect on online anti-Indian volume is measured only by ISD's 2023→2024 rise. That rise mixes the diplomatic row with the housing debate.

## 3. Hate-crime and harassment statistics

### Takeaway
Official statistics exist but are coarse. The US has no "anti-Indian" category: incidents fall under anti-Asian, anti-Hindu or anti-Sikh bias. Canada's police-reported crimes against South Asians rose sharply in 2024 and 2025. UK national data counts Sikhs inside broader categories. All official counts are believed to undercount.

### Cited Findings

**United States: FBI data**
- **FBI 2024 data (released Aug 2025):**
  - **11,679** hate incidents in total;
  - **142 anti-Sikh** incidents, the third-highest religious category after anti-Jewish (1,938) and anti-Muslim (228);
  - **25 anti-Hindu**;
  - about **379 anti-Asian** (one analysis).
  - Status: reported (secondary summaries of the FBI release; check against the FBI Crime Data Explorer).
  - Sources: [Arab American Institute](https://www.aaiusa.org/library/2024-hate-crime-data); [Asian Americans Advancing Justice–AAJC](https://www.advancingjustice-aajc.org/press-release/fbi-hate-crime-data-reveals-persistent-threats-asian-americans-amid-escalating-anti); [AAJC report PDF, Nov 2025](https://www.advancingjustice-aajc.org/sites/default/files/2025-11/Anti-Asian%20Hate%20in%202024%20Report.pdf).
- **2025 preliminary data:** anti-Sikh incidents reached a **record 177**, and anti-Hindu rose to **28** (+12%), while overall hate crimes fell. The analysis is by Brian Levin, who warns the figures are **preliminary** and may change. Status: reported, preliminary. Sources: [Axios, 9 Apr 2026](https://axios.com/2026/04/09/hate-crime-data-latinos-sikhs-2025); [Christian Today India](https://www.christiantoday.co.in/world/fbi-data-us-hate-crimes-decline-overall-but-anti-sikh-and-anti-hindu-incidents-climb-in-2025.html).
- **Limits:**
  - There is **no national figure for Indian Americans as such**.
  - FBI reporting by local agencies is voluntary and uneven.
  - Advocates say most bias incidents are never reported.
  - Status: confirmed as a limitation. Source: [AAJC](https://www.advancingjustice-aajc.org/press-release/fbi-hate-crime-data-reveals-persistent-threats-asian-americans-amid-escalating-anti).
- **Survey data:** **48% of South Asian adults** reported a hate act in 2025 (Stop AAPI Hate national survey). Status: reported. Source: [India West](https://indiawest.com/anti-south-asian-slurs-surge-109-in-us/).
- **SAALT:** no recent SAALT data was found in this session (see Gaps).

**Canada: Statistics Canada**
- **Police-reported hate crimes:**
  - **2024 (released 30 Mar 2026):** race- or ethnicity-motivated hate crimes rose **8%**. Crimes against **South Asian** populations rose **15%, to 321 incidents**.
  - **2025 (table released 22 Jul 2026):** South Asian-targeted hate crimes reached **483, up 49%**, concentrated in **Ontario and British Columbia**.
  - Status: confirmed (official StatCan). Note that StatCan counts include confirmed and suspected incidents.
  - Sources: [StatCan Daily, 30 Mar 2026](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/260330/dq260330a-eng.htm); [StatCan chart](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/260330/cg-a004-eng.htm); [StatCan table 2021–2025](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/260722/t006a-eng.htm); [StatCan infographic](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/11-627-m/11-627-m2026005-eng.htm).
- **Toronto Police:** anti-South Asian hate crimes rose from **14 (2023) to 41 (2024)**. The service suggested the rise "may be linked to anti-immigration" sentiment. Status: reported (the source text was cut off). Source: [Toronto Police 2024 annual hate crime report](https://www.tps.ca/media/filer_public/5d/b2/5db216d5-a6f9-4f78-8e96-6187137cad3e/96c49e87-24c0-4800-a4ae-442a843fb7f1.pdf).

**United Kingdom**
- **Home Office counts:** racially aggravated hate crime totalled about 70,000 (Apr 2024 – Mar 2025), according to the Sikh Federation's evidence to Parliament. **Sikhs are not recorded as a separate ethnic category**, and Sikh groups want separate recording. Status: reported (an advocacy submission to Parliament; check the figure against Home Office tables). Source: [Sikh Federation evidence to Parliament](https://committees.parliament.uk/writtenevidence/138005/pdf/).
- **British Sikh Report 2025:** **54%** of respondents fear their community could be misunderstood or targeted. This is a perception survey. Status: reported. Source: [British Sikh Report 2025](https://britishsikhreport.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/British-Sikh-Report-2025.pdf).

### Inferences
- Canada has the clearest **official** signal that is consistent with the online trend: South Asian-targeted police-reported hate crimes went from 321 in 2024 to 483 in 2025.
- In the US the official numbers are small (tens to low hundreds), while the online numbers run to hundreds of thousands. The film must not imply that online volume equals offline crime.

### Gaps
- **SAALT:** no current reports found (it has published on hate violence against South Asians in earlier years). Not reached this session.
- **Australia:** no official national hate-crime statistics by ethnicity were found. Scanlon Foundation and Australian Human Rights Commission data were not reached.
- **Ireland:** Garda hate-crime statistics by victim nationality for 2025 were not found.
- **UK:** Home Office 2024/25 tables were not checked directly. No breakdown for people of Indian ethnicity was found.

## 4. Academic research: economic anxiety, perceived competition, scapegoating

### Takeaway
The classic intuition is that "they take our jobs", in academic terms realistic group conflict and labour-market competition. It has **surprisingly weak support** as an explanation of how individuals feel about immigration. Better-supported explanations are **sociotropic and cultural concerns**, **status threat**, and **"politicized places"**: a local change in visibility combined with national threat rhetoric. These help explain why the hostility spikes around the news and is aimed at visible, successful groups.

### Cited Findings
- **Hainmueller & Hopkins (2014), *Annual Review of Political Science*, "Public Attitudes Toward Immigration":** the review concludes that **labour-market competition gets weak empirical support** as a driver of mass attitudes. **Sociotropic and cultural concerns** (beliefs about effects on the nation) explain more. Status: confirmed (peer-reviewed). Sources: [PDF via refugee-economies.org](https://www.refugee-economies.org/assets/downloads/Journal_Hainmueller_and_Hopkins.pdf); [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/314511421_Public_Attitudes_toward_Immigration).
- **Hainmueller & Hopkins conjoint study, "The Hidden American Immigration Consensus":**
  - Americans of all education levels and parties **prefer highly educated migrants in skilled jobs**. Low-skilled Americans do not oppose low-skilled migrants more, which is what job competition would predict.
  - Status: confirmed (peer-reviewed; published in AJPS, 2015).
  - Source: [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/256025282_The_Hidden_American_Immigration_Consensus_A_Conjoint_Analysis_of_Attitudes_Toward_Immigrants).
  - **This matters for the film.** The online anti-Indian narrative targets high-skilled H-1B workers. Survey evidence says the US public generally *favours* skilled migrants, so the online hostility may come from a **minority, not public opinion**. That is an inference; see the Inferences below.
- **Labour-market competition, mixed evidence:**
  - A 2026 review by Alexander Kustov calls the labour-competition hypothesis something of a "zombie theory" that keeps failing tests. It also notes that skill specificity and job availability may matter in some occupations.
  - Some European studies still find that perceived economic effects drive anti-immigrant attitudes.
  - Status: contested (an active academic debate).
  - Sources: [Kustov, Immigration Attitudes Review 2026](https://alexanderkustov.org/assets/iar/review-v2026.1.html); [Oxford ORA: "Skill Specificity and Attitudes towards Immigration"](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid:08533dcd-a8aa-4e0e-947f-cb5b16502777/files/mf5a968986f4d184ecaa42a56bd0939e7); ["They take our jobs and reduce our wages" (ResearchGate)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/371139389_They_take_our_jobs_and_reduce_our_wages_attitudes_toward_immigrants_in_Europe); ["Do Concerns About Labor Market Competition Shape Attitudes Toward Immigration? New Evidence"](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/228162832_Do_Concerns_About_Labor_Market_Competition_Shape_Attitudes_Toward_Immigration_New_Evidence).
- **Group conflict theory tested longitudinally in Europe, 2002–2016:** the theory holds that hostility grows when groups compete for scarce resources. Status: confirmed that the study exists; read the paper for its result before quoting it. Source: [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/335550803_Anti-Immigration_Attitudes_in_Europe_2002-2016_A_Longitudinal_Test_of_the_Group_Conflict_Theory).
- **Hopkins (2010), *American Political Science Review* 104(1):40–60, "Politicized Places: Explaining Where and When Immigrants Provoke Local Opposition":**
  - **Argument:** anti-immigrant attitudes are strongest where **rapid local influx** and **salient national threat rhetoric** coincide.
  - **Evidence:** 12 geocoded surveys, 1992–2009.
  - **Numbers, from Journalist's Resource's summary:**
    - when immigration is not in the national headlines, residents of fast-changing counties are only **0.1%** more likely to want less immigration;
    - during high national attention the gap is **9.9–15.1%**;
    - **58%** of local anti-immigrant proposals in 2000–2006 came during or right after national immigration debates.
  - Status: confirmed (peer-reviewed); the specific figures are reported by the summary.
  - Sources: [Cambridge/APSR](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/american-political-science-review/article/abs/politicized-places-explaining-where-and-when-immigrants-provoke-local-opposition/2E1DEBDEA57191252E61B41084AFA348); [Journalist's Resource](https://journalistsresource.org/politics-and-government/politicized-provoke-opposition/); [PDF](https://refugee-economies.org/assets/downloads/Journal_Hopkins.pdf).
  - A follow-up by Fetzer in Miami, Marseille and Dublin largely confirmed it. Status: reported. Source: [Journalist's Resource](https://journalistsresource.org/politics-and-government/politicized-provoke-opposition/).
- **Mutz (2018), PNAS, "Status threat, not economic hardship, explains the 2016 presidential vote":**
  - Mutz found that perceived threat to the status of dominant groups (including from trade, immigration and China) predicted Trump support better than personal economic hardship. Status: confirmed that the paper exists.
  - Sources: [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/324708462_Status_threat_not_economic_hardship_explains_the_2016_presidential_vote); [Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Status-threat,-not-economic-hardship,-explains-the-Mutz/032bed07a5e4e68f438fd7ba380f214b36c3777a).
  - **Contested:** Stephen Morgan's reanalysis argues that Mutz overstated how well the data separates status threat from material interest. Sources: [Morgan reanalysis PDF](https://statmodeling.stat.columbia.edu/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/Morgan-Fake-News-Mutz-Reanalysis-20180510.pdf); [Gelman blog discussion](https://statmodeling.stat.columbia.edu/2018/05/14/status-threat-explain-2016-presidential-vote/).

### Inferences
- **A plausible, sourced chain for the film:** people of Indian origin have become **visible** in high-status roles: tech CEOs, H-1B holders (most H-1B approvals go to Indian nationals; *unverified this session, see Gaps*), and politicians such as Ramaswamy, Krishnan and Mamdani. When **national rhetoric** frames them as a threat (H-1B fights, tariffs, migration-number flyers), hostility rises (Hopkins). The economic framing ("taking tech jobs") is the respectable surface, but the research suggests **status and cultural threat** do more of the work than measured job loss (Hainmueller & Hopkins; Mutz, though contested).
- This links to NCRI's finding that slurs moved to "policy framing": the economic argument can be a vehicle for the hostility rather than its root. **This is an interpretation. Present it as what the research suggests, not as proven for this case.**
- The conjoint finding (the public prefers skilled migrants) supports the "loud minority" reading of online data: a few amplifiers (NCRI) are not the same as broad public opinion.

### Gaps
- **Classic theory sources** were not retrieved this session: Sherif's Robbers Cave / realistic conflict theory (1954/1961), Allport's *The Nature of Prejudice* (1954), and the frustration–aggression and scapegoat theory of Hovland & Sears (1940). Add primary citations before using them.
- **Labour-market effects of H-1B:** no studies were retrieved (e.g. Peri, Shih & Sparber on H-1B and native wages and employment). The "do H-1Bs actually lower wages?" counter-evidence needs its own research pass. It is contested: EPI-type critiques against economists who find positive or neutral effects.
- **H-1B shares:** the share of H-1B approvals going to Indian nationals (USCIS data, often reported at about 70%+) was **not verified this session**.
- No peer-reviewed study **specific to anti-Indian online hate** was found.

## 5. Specific narratives: "pajeet", "Indians taking tech jobs", "Indian scammer" tropes

### Takeaway
"Pajeet" began on 4chan's /int/ board in July 2015, mocking Indian sanitation and culture. It is now the most-used anti-Asian slur in the extremist spaces Moonshot monitors and appears in about 30% of anti-South Asian X posts (CSOH). The "job thief / invader" narrative is the largest category (about 45% to nearly 70% depending on the dataset). The "scammer" trope appears in coverage, but I found no study measuring it on its own.

### Cited Findings
- **Origin of "pajeet":**
  - The earliest known use is a **July 2015 thread on 4chan's /int/ (international) board**. It was modelled on the "Mehmet, my son" meme of late 2014, which mocked Turkish people. Variants include "Pajeeta" and "Pajeet Kumar."
  - The early threads mocked **Indian sanitation** (e.g. "designated shitting streets") and the "India smells bad" jokes.
  - The proposed etymologies ("-jeet" from Sanskrit/Punjabi names, "paji") are speculative.
  - The term has spread more widely since the early 2020s.
  - Status: reported (meme and wiki sources, which are weak for academic claims but consistent with each other; confirmed in substance by Stop AAPI Hate's own statement that it began on 4chan in 2015).
  - Sources: [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/sensitive/memes/pajeet); [KYM explainer](https://knowyourmeme.com/editorials/guides/what-is-the-pajeet-kumar-meme-the-derogatory-4chan-term-used-to-describe-indians-explained); [Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/pajeet); [Wikipedia: Pajeet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pajeet); [Stop AAPI Hate, Aug 2025](https://stopaapihate.org/2025/08/07/keeping-count-behind-the-spike-in-anti-south-asian-hate/).
- **How common it is now:**
  - "pajeet" appears in **nearly 30%** of anti-South Asian hate posts on X (CSOH, 2025–26);
  - it is the **most commonly used anti-Asian slur** in extremist online spaces (Stop AAPI Hate/Moonshot, 2025);
  - it is among the slurs in NCRI's peak of **800+ posts a week** (Dec 2025), alongside "dothead."
  - Status: reported.
  - Sources: [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/28/anti-south-asian-hate-speech-has-exploded-online-in-us-new-report-finds); [Stop AAPI Hate](https://stopaapihate.org/2025/08/07/keeping-count-behind-the-spike-in-anti-south-asian-hate/); [NCRI](https://networkcontagion.us/reports/from-policy-drift-to-purity-grift-how-a-small-network-hijacked-the-immigration-debate/).
- **Hygiene dehumanisation** (the original 4chan theme) is the **fastest-growing category, +171%** (CSOH 2026). Status: reported. Source: [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/28/anti-south-asian-hate-speech-has-exploded-online-in-us-new-report-finds).
- **"Invaders" and "job thieves":**
  - about **45%** of anti-South Asian hate posts on X (CSOH 2026);
  - immigration and expulsion themes made up **nearly 70%** of the high-engagement anti-Indian posts in Jul–Sep 2025 (CSOH 2025);
  - NCRI documents the move from slurs to "replacement" and policy framing in mid-2025.
  - Status: reported.
  - Sources: [CSOH 2026](https://www.csohate.org/2026/09/28/anti-south-asian-hate-united-states-2026/); [American Bazaar on CSOH 2025](https://americanbazaaronline.com/2025/09/18/anti-indian-racism-on-x-on-the-rise-report-467757/); [Business Standard on NCRI](https://www.business-standard.com/immigration/indians-in-us-called-invaders-scammers-hate-posts-on-x-drew-300-mn-views-126031200623_1.html).
- **The "scammer" trope:**
  - Business Standard's headline on the NCRI report ("Indians in US called invaders, scammers?") shows the trope is present in the data. Status: reported. Source: [Business Standard, 12 Mar 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/immigration/indians-in-us-called-invaders-scammers-hate-posts-on-x-drew-300-mn-views-126031200623_1.html).
  - Commentary describes memes about Indian tech support and accents, framed as "jokes." Status: opinion. Source: [Substack "Normalize Indian Hate"](https://historycanthide.substack.com/p/normalize-indian-hate-gen-zs-problem).
  - Real tech-support fraud from call centres in India is a documented criminal industry. One academic article examines it: "India's call-center frauds: a neocolonial perspective on organized crime" (Univ. of Portsmouth). Only the title was seen.
  - **Counter-point:** Microsoft's survey found that **Indians themselves had the highest tech-support-scam encounter rate (69%)** in its 2021 global survey. In other words, Indians are major *victims* too.
  - Status: reported.
  - Sources: [Portsmouth research portal](https://researchportal.port.ac.uk/en/publications/indias-call-center-frauds-a-neocolonial-perspective-on-organized-/); [Microsoft India Tech Support Fraud Survey 2021 (PDF)](https://news.microsoft.com/wp-content/uploads/prod/sites/45/2021/07/Microsoft-India-Tech-Support-Fraud-Survey-2021.pdf); [TechRadar](https://techradar.com/news/indians-are-global-toppers-in-getting-conned-in-support-related-scams).
- **AI-generated caricatures:** CSOH names generative AI as a new amplifier of racist imagery (2026). Status: reported. Source: [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/28/anti-south-asian-hate-speech-has-exploded-online-in-us-new-report-finds).
- **"Go back to your country" framing** (an AAPI Equity Alliance explainer on anti-Indian sentiment on US social media). Status: opinion/advocacy. Source: [AAPI Equity Alliance](https://aapiequityalliance.org/go-back-to-your-country-the-surge-of-anti-indian-sentiment-on-american-social-media/).

### Inferences
- The narrative stack is layered: **hygiene/dehumanisation** (2015 4chan origin, still growing), **scam/fraud** (based on a real criminal industry that is generalised to a whole people), and **jobs/invasion** (the newest and largest layer, tied to H-1B and migration numbers). Each layer starts from a real-world hook (sanitation statistics, scam call centres, H-1B shares) and turns it into a trait of a whole population. This is the scapegoating mechanism in Section 4.
- Because the film is also about why India is mocked, it must handle real issues (sanitation, scam centres) **without endorsing the generalisation**. The Microsoft finding that Indians are top scam victims is a strong, neutral counterweight.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed etymology or diffusion study of "pajeet" was found. The origin rests on meme and wiki sources plus Stop AAPI Hate's statement.
- No quantified share of the "scammer" trope in any monitoring dataset was found.
- No data was found on how much of the anti-Indian content comes from **outside** the target country, such as foreign or bot networks. NCRI's concentration finding hints at this but does not settle it.
- No India-side measurement was found, such as Indian accounts' counter-mobilisation or retaliatory content, which could inflate the "hate" counts through quote-posts.
