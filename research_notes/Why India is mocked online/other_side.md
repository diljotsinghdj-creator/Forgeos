# The other side: legitimate criticisms and complicating factors (online stereotyping of India)

Research date: 2026-11-17. Status key: **confirmed** (primary or official source, or several independent reputable outlets agree); **reported** (a single reputable outlet or secondary coverage, primary not opened); **contested** (sources disagree); **opinion** (commentary or advocacy).
Method note: most figures below come from news and secondary coverage of primary reports (NFHS-5, IQAir, IC3, CPCB, FSSAI). I did not open every primary PDF. Anything marked "reported" should be checked against the primary document before it goes on screen.

## Q1. Real sanitation, pollution and public-health data

### Takeaway
The problems the stereotype exaggerates are real and measurable: about 19% of households had no toilet in NFHS-5 even after India was declared open-defecation-free in 2019; India is the 6th most polluted country for PM2.5 (IQAir 2025), with Loni and Delhi in the global top 5; the Ganga and Yamuna fail bathing standards at key points; a large share of waste is unprocessed; and roughly a fifth of food samples fail FSSAI standards. Each figure also has a trend or a definitional caveat that the clips leave out.

### Cited Findings
**Sanitation (Swachh Bharat / ODF)**
- India was declared ODF on 2 October 2019 on the basis of self-declarations by villages and districts, not a survey of toilet use. The government's own answer to Parliament (Rajya Sabha, 1 Aug 2022) — [Rajya Sabha PQ 257](https://rsdebate.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/731536/1/PQ_257_01082022_U1691_p390_p391.pdf). Status: confirmed (official document, reported via search summary).
- NFHS-5 (2019–21): "Nineteen percent of households have no facility, which means that the household members practice open defecation". Toilet access was lowest in Bihar (62%), Jharkhand (70%) and Odisha (71%) — [Deccan Herald, May 2022](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/19-households-do-not-use-any-toilet-facility-nfhs-survey-1108334.html); [The Wire](https://thewire.in/government/nfhs-5-sanitation-open-defecation). Status: confirmed (multiple outlets quoting the NFHS-5 report).
- A fact-check quotes an expert saying NFHS-5 asked about toilet access, while the government's NARSS survey also asked about usability, safety and waste management, which may explain part of the gap with official ODF claims. The same fact-check gives about 26% for rural households in the 2019–21 report — [The Quint WebQoof](https://www.thequint.com/news/webqoof/prime-minister-narendra-modi-claim-indian-villages-open-defecation-free-fact-check). Status: contested (indicators differ).
- IndiaSpend notes a September 2019 study suggesting open defecation may be underestimated, depending on how survey questions are asked — [IndiaSpend](https://www.indiaspend.com/data-gaps/access-to-toilets-sanitation-swachh-bharat-abhiyan-data-surveys-762488). Status: reported.
- Balance: the same NFHS series shows a large rise in toilet access (The Wire gives rural improved sanitation rising from 37% to 65% between NFHS-4 and NFHS-5; check the exact indicator) — [The Wire](https://thewire.in/government/nfhs-5-sanitation-open-defecation). Status: reported.

**Air quality**
- IQAir World Air Quality Report 2025 (released March 2026): India ranked 6th of 143 countries, with a national average PM2.5 of 48.9 µg/m³, about 10 times the WHO annual guideline of 5 µg/m³ — [The Wire](https://thewire.in/environment/a-2025-world-air-quality-report-lists-india-as-the-sixth-most-polluted-country); [Business Today, 24 Mar 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/air-quality-report-2025-this-up-city-named-worlds-most-polluted-pakistan-bangladesh-tajikistan-and-india-among-top-522153-2026-03-24). Status: reported (primary IQAir PDF not opened).
- Loni (Uttar Pradesh) was the world's most polluted city and Delhi was 4th; five of the ten most polluted cities were Indian (Loni, Byrnihat, Delhi, Ghaziabad, Ula). New Delhi was the most polluted capital for the 8th year running — [DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/news/national/india-6th-most-polluted-country-ups-loni-worlds-most-polluted-city-delhi-4th-report); [Tribune India](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/indias-5-cities-among-worlds-top-10-most-polluted-places/). Status: reported.
- Trend: India was 3rd in 2023, 5th in 2024 and 6th in 2025, a slow improvement in rank — [IAS Gyan summary](https://www.iasgyan.in/daily-current-affairs/india-ranks-6th-in-world-air-quality-report-2025). Status: reported (exam-prep site; confirm with IQAir).
- Counts of Indian cities in the world's 100 most polluted differ by source and year (66 vs 74 of 100; 83 in 2023) — [Outlook India](https://www.outlookindia.com/national/india-ranked-6th-most-polluted-country-ups-loni-most-polluted-city-globally-report). Status: contested (year mix-up likely). Do not use a single number without the IQAir PDF.
- Monitoring caveat: about 1,600 stations, heavily skewed to cities — [Drishti IAS](https://www.drishtiias.com/state-pcs-current-affairs/world-air-quality-report-2025/print_manually). Status: reported.

**Ganga and Yamuna**
- During the Maha Kumbh (Feb 2025), the CPCB told the National Green Tribunal that water at Prayagraj was not fit for bathing because of high faecal coliform at the monitored sites (five on the Ganga, two on the Yamuna) — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/uttar-pradesh/high-faecal-coliform-levels-in-prayagraj-during-maha-kumbh-cpcb-report-to-ngt-3409562); [Newslaundry, 18 Feb 2025](https://www.newslaundry.com/amp/story/2025%2F02%2F18%2Fmahakumbh-waters-unsafe-for-bathing-a-report-finds-faecal-bacteria-13-times-over-safe-limit). Status: confirmed (CPCB filing to NGT, widely reported). The bathing limit is 2,500 MPN/100 ml (desirable 500).
- Balance: a later CPCB analysis reported in the press said river quality was "better" overall during the Kumbh while faecal contamination persisted. The government disputed the early readings — [Punjab Kesari English](https://english.punjabkesari.com/india/2025/02/18/mahakumbh-cpcb-reports-better-river-quality-fecal-contamination-issues-persist). Status: contested.
- The CPCB has identified about 296–311 polluted river stretches nationally (figures differ by report year: 311 in 2022, 296 in the 2022–23 review) — [Down To Earth](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/water/maha-kumbh-2025-ganga-yamunas-long-term-sustainability-depends-on-continuous-monitoring-improved-wastewater-treatment-and-public-cooperation); [CPCB report to NGT](https://www.greentribunal.gov.in/sites/default/files/news_updates/Report%20by%20CPCB%20(Page%20No.%2039007-39113).pdf). Status: reported (confirm with the CPCB primary).
- Yamuna in Delhi: monthly DPCC/CPCB data reported spikes in faecal coliform far above safe limits — [The Wire](https://m.thewire.in/environment/yamuna-water-quality-dips-as-levels-of-faecal-bacteria-spike-4000-times-above-safe-limit-report). Status: reported (check the month and figure before use).

**Waste management**
- A Union minister told Parliament that 73% of India's solid waste is processed. CPCB's 2020–21 annual report put the figure at about 47%, with about 27% landfilled and 26% unaccounted for — [Scroll](https://scroll.in/article/1030113/a-union-minister-told-parliament-that-73-of-indias-solid-waste-is-processed-how-true-is-this); [FactChecker](https://www.factchecker.in/context-check/does-india-really-process-73-of-its-waste-as-union-minister-claims-829905). Status: contested (official versus regulator data).
- CSE's legacy-waste analysis: more than half of the waste generated in 2019–20 was dumped or left unattended; over 80% of legacy waste is unremediated in 15 states and union territories — [Down To Earth / CSE](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/waste/neglect-of-sustainable-scientific-waste-treatment-behind-india-s-unscientific-landfills-cse-analysis-84942); [Waste Recycling Mag](https://www.wasterecyclingmag.com/news/over-80-of-legacy-waste-remains-unremediated-in-15-states-and-union-territories-cse-report). Status: reported.

**Food safety (FSSAI)**
- 2018–19: of 106,459 samples analysed, 30,415 (28.5%) were non-conforming — [FSSAI press release, 26 Nov 2019](https://mail.fssai.gov.in/upload/press_release/2019/11/5ddce90b2867fPress_Release_Enforcement_Data_26_11_2019.pdf); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/current/economy-politics/20-percent-of-1-lakh-food-samples-did-not-conform-to-quality-in-2018-19-says-food-regulator/story/391016.html). Status: confirmed (primary FSSAI release).
- "Non-conforming" is not the same as "adulterated": it combines unsafe, substandard and misbranded samples. In one Haryana breakdown, 737 of 2,929 samples were non-conforming, of which only 95 were "unsafe" — [FoodNavigator-Asia, Jan 2020](https://www.foodnavigator-asia.com/Article/2020/01/21/India-food-quality-Over-one-in-four-samples-fail-quality-test-as-FSSAI-makes-improvement-claims). Status: confirmed. Important for fairness: headlines that say "28% adulterated" overstate it.
- FY2024–25 (answer to Parliament): 170,535 samples tested, 34,388 non-conforming (about 20%) — [Lok Sabha answer](https://eparlib.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/953391/1/AU3357.pdf). Status: reported (matched by search summary; verify the document number).
- FY2025–26 milk and dairy: 14,668 of 39,351 samples (37.3%) non-conforming. The government called the figures provisional — [Rural Voice](https://eng.ruralvoice.in/milk-quality-concerns-deepen-37-pc-of-samples-fail-fssai-standards-in-fy26-over-11700-cases-filed). Status: reported.

### Inferences
- The fair framing is "real problems, unevenly distributed and improving in places, but clipped without context". The film should show at least one hard number per category on screen before it critiques the mockery.
- The ODF gap (declaration against survey) is a strong evidence beat: it is the government's own data that complicates the government's own claim, so it is not partisan.

### Gaps
- I did not open the primary NFHS-5, IQAir, CPCB or FSSAI PDFs. Exact figures need checking before they are shown on screen.
- No 2026 Delhi winter AQI peak was retrieved. If one is needed, pull CPCB daily AQI bulletins for the specific date.
- No WHO country-level PM2.5 mortality estimate was retrieved.

## Q2. Scam call centres

### Takeaway
Indian tech-support scam operations are well documented by the CBI and FBI themselves (Operation Chakra, 2022 onwards), and US tech-support fraud losses reached about $2.1 billion in 2025 (IC3). That is a factual basis for the stereotype. However, Indian police are the ones dismantling these centres, and Microsoft's own survey found Indians among the most frequently targeted and defrauded by the same scams.

### Cited Findings
- The CBI launched the first Operation Chakra in October 2022, searching 115 locations linked to financial cybercrime, in coordination with the FBI, Interpol and others — [GovInfoSecurity](https://www.govinfosecurity.com/indian-federal-police-raid-tech-scam-centers-a-23373). Status: confirmed (widely reported CBI action).
- Microsoft says it helped the CBI dismantle Indian call centres in 2025 — [The Hacker News, June 2025](https://thehackernews.com/2025/06/microsoft-helps-cbi-dismantle-indian.html?hl=en_US). Status: reported.
- Operation Chakra-V (July 2025): the CBI raided a Noida call centre ("FirstIdea") accused of defrauding UK and Australian victims of about £390,000. The FBI, the UK National Crime Agency and Microsoft helped. Two people were arrested, including the alleged ringleader — [The Hacker News, July 2025](https://thehackernews.com/2025/07/cbi-shuts-down-390k-uk-tech-support.html); [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/world/asia/india-cbi-cybercrime-scam-uk-australia). Status: reported (allegations; no court finding).
- May 2025: the CBI and Japanese police searched 19 locations in Delhi, Haryana and UP over a scam targeting Japanese nationals, and arrested six people — [Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/cbi-japan-police-bust-tech-support-call-centre-scam-targeting-japanese-nationals-6-arrested/77272.html). Status: reported.
- December 2025: the CBI, acting on FBI inputs, raided a Noida call centre and arrested six people. The FBI said IC3 complaints tied more than $48.7 million in losses from over 600 US victims to the network. The CBI's own estimate was about $8.5 million. The US Embassy praised the cooperation — [The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/india/cbi-busts-transnational-cybercrime-racket-in-noida-with-fbi-help-us-embassy-hails-us-india-partnership-1503526840.html); [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fus-thanks-cbi-on-busting-illegal-call-centre-that-duped-its-citizens-of-millions-3700041). Status: contested (FBI and CBI loss figures differ; allegations only).
- FBI IC3 2025 Internet Crime Report (April 2026): tech and customer support fraud losses of about $2.13 billion (up from $1.46 billion) from 47,794 complaints. People aged 60+ reported $7.75 billion in total losses from 201,266 complaints (up 59%), and tech-support fraud was the second-largest category for them at about $1.04 billion — [AARP](https://www.aarp.org/money/scams-fraud/fbi-ftc-report-2025-losses.html); [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/fbi-seniors-cybercrime-2025/). Status: reported (primary IC3 PDF not opened. **IC3 does not attribute all tech-support losses to India**; do not imply it does).
- Microsoft Global Tech Support Scam Research 2021 (YouGov, 16,254 adults in 16 countries): 69% of Indian respondents encountered a tech-support scam in the past year, the highest rate globally; 48% of Indian respondents continued engaging with the scam (global average 16%) — [Microsoft India press release, July 2021](https://news.microsoft.com/en-in/seven-out-of-10-consumers-in-india-encountered-tech-support-scams-in-the-past-year-microsoft-survey/); [survey PDF](https://news.microsoft.com/wp-content/uploads/prod/sites/45/2021/07/Microsoft-India-Tech-Support-Fraud-Survey-2021.pdf). Status: confirmed (primary). Caveat: from 2021, the latest edition found.
- A Microsoft study of 61 tech-support scams found most appeared to be located in India — reported in [GovInfoSecurity](https://www.govinfosecurity.com/indian-federal-police-raid-tech-scam-centers-a-23373). Status: reported (study date and sample not verified).

### Inferences
- The scam stereotype has a real, documented core: a concentration of operations in the NCR (Noida, Gurugram, Delhi). The fair counterpoint is that this is an organised-crime industry employing a tiny fraction of people, that Indian agencies prosecute it, and that Indians are among its largest victim groups.
- Strong visual pairing: an FBI/IC3 loss figure next to the Microsoft India victim figure. Same crime, both sides as victims.

### Gaps
- No count of total Indian employees in scam centres exists; do not estimate one.
- No source found on Indian victims' total rupee losses specifically from tech-support scams. I4C/NCRP figures for all cyber fraud could be a substitute (not retrieved).
- Microsoft's 61-scam study: original publication not located.

## Q3. Indian creators and media producing the same content

### Takeaway
Some "dirty India" content is made and consumed domestically: Indian creators post "most unhygienic street food" videos, Indian TV news has been sanctioned by its own self-regulator for sensational and unverified content, and fact-checkers have found some viral "disgusting food" clips to be AI-generated. Evidence here is thinner and more anecdotal than for Q1–Q2.

### Cited Findings
- Example of an Indian creator's video titled "I Tried India's Most Unhygienic Street Food (Shocking Results)" — [Recapio digest](https://recapio.com/digest/i-tried-indias-most-unhygienic-street-food-shocking-results-by-sahilxp). Status: reported (a digest of the video, not a study. Verify on YouTube before showing).
- Indian forum users describe "a whole genre of shock entertainment" showing gross food conditions — [TechEnclave thread](https://techenclave.com/t/what-do-you-think-about-the-street-food-videos-being-made-of-india/272300). Status: opinion (forum).
- Correctiv (Germany) found that viral "disgust" videos of food preparation in India were AI-generated (13 May 2026) — [Correctiv](https://correctiv.org/faktencheck/2026/05/13/ekel-fuer-klicks-diese-videos-von-essen-in-indien-sind-ki-generiert/). A viral clip of rats crawling over snacks was also found to be AI-generated — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/viral/fact-check-ai-generated-video-of-rats-crawling-on-food-misleads-netizens-sparks-concern-about-hygiene). Status: confirmed (fact-checks).
- Some Indian creators push back: one took a Chinese commentator who called Indian food "dirty" out to eat — [Zee News](https://embed2.zeenews.com/viral/watch-youtuber-takes-chinese-woman-to-lunch-after-dirty-indian-food-claim-2812633.html). Status: reported.
- Indian TV news: a Newslaundry analysis of 416 complaints to the NBDSA (the industry self-regulator) since 2019 found just 12 fines, a median of Rs 50,000 (5 Dec 2025) — [Newslaundry](https://www.newslaundry.com/2025/12/05/5-years-416-complaints-just-12-fines-the-mute-button-of-indian-news-tvs-first-self-regulator). Status: reported (Newslaundry is a media critic; figures are its own analysis).
- The NBDSA fined Zee News Rs 1 lakh for an unverified viral video that blamed a community for a traffic jam later shown to be caused by a landslide — [The Federal](https://thefederal.com/category/news/zee-news-fined-fake-viral-video-journalism-crisis-discussion-230692). The NBDSA's June 2025 advisory told all broadcasters that tickers and thumbnails must match the actual discussion — [SabrangIndia](https://sabrangindia.in/nbdsa-issued-advisory-to-all-broadcasters-tickers-and-thumbnail-should-conform-to-the-actual-of-the-discussions). Status: confirmed (regulator orders, reported).
- Reuters Institute Digital News Report 2022: 41% of Indian respondents trusted news overall; the report described some Indian TV channels as "infamous for sensationalising news" — [The Quint](https://www.thequint.com/amp/story/news/india/reuters-institute-digital-news-report-news-trustworthiness-digital-brands-global-trends). Status: reported (2022; the sample is English-speaking and online).

### Inferences
- The "algorithm rewards outrage" thesis is not only about foreign audiences. Domestic creators and channels compete for the same engagement. This supports a structural (incentive) explanation more than a purely racial one.
- AI-generated "dirty food" videos cut both ways: they show that some mockery is fabricated, but they also show demand for this content that the film must not wave away.

### Gaps
- I found no peer-reviewed study measuring how much "dirty India" content is made by Indian creators versus foreign creators. Do not claim a share.
- "Indian users mocking other Indians" (regional, caste or class mockery online) was not sourced in this round; it needs a separate search (e.g. studies of regional slurs, "Bihari" jokes).

## Q4. Internal prejudice

### Takeaway
India and its diaspora have well-documented internal prejudices: caste discrimination (now a live legal issue in the US), a long-running skin-lightening industry, and racist violence against Northeast Indians and Africans in India. Present each with both sides' positions.

### Cited Findings
**Caste in the diaspora**
- In February 2023, Seattle became the first US city to add caste to its anti-discrimination laws; Fresno later followed — [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/employment/california-caste-bill-reveals-broad-tensions-over-fighting-bias); [Bloomberg Tax](https://news.bloombergtax.com/daily-tax-report-international/seattles-new-caste-bias-ban-renews-calls-for-federal-guidance). Status: confirmed.
- California SB 403 passed the Assembly 55–3 and the Senate 31–5, but Governor Newsom vetoed it in October 2023, calling it "unnecessary" because caste is already covered by existing protected categories (such as ancestry) — [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/legal-ops-and-tech/newsom-vetoes-californias-first-in-nation-ban-on-caste-bias); [HR Brew](https://www.hr-brew.com/stories/2023/10/18/advocates-are-divided-on-california-governor-gavin-newsom-s-veto-of-a-caste-discrimination-bill). Status: confirmed. Both sides: Equality Labs and Dalit groups backed the bill; the Hindu American Foundation and others opposed it as singling out Hindus and South Asians.
- Cisco case: California's civil rights agency (then DFEH, now the Civil Rights Department) sued Cisco in 2020 on behalf of a Dalit engineer who alleged lower pay, fewer opportunities and retaliation. In April 2023, the department voluntarily dismissed claims against the two supervisors (Sundar Iyer and Ramana Kompella) while continuing the case against Cisco. Cisco has disputed the claims — [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/asian-america/calif-scraps-caste-bias-case-cisco-engineers-company-still-sued-rcna79434); [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2023/04/11/cisco-caste-discrimination-case-dismissed/). Status: confirmed (as of 2023). The HAF called the dismissal a vindication; Equality Labs called it "progress" — [same NBC article]. Status: opinion (both sides).
- **Flag:** I could not find any 2024–2026 ruling or settlement in the Cisco case against the company. Its current status is **unconfirmed**; do not say "ongoing" or "resolved" without checking the docket.

**Colour prejudice**
- Hindustan Unilever announced on 25 June 2020 that it would drop "Fair" from Fair & Lovely; it was renamed Glow & Lovely on 2 July 2020 (men's range: Glow & Handsome). The move followed Unilever's global removal of "white/whitening, light/lightening, fair/fairness", amid Black Lives Matter protests; India was the brand's biggest market — [Scroll](https://scroll.in/latest/966333/scroll_in); [Outlook India](https://www.outlookindia.com/business/business-news-hindustan-unilevers-fair-lovely-to-be-renamed-glow-lovely-news-355887); [The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/biz-tech/2020/06/25/fair-and-lovely-to-be-renamed-as-glow-and-lovely.amp.html). Status: confirmed. Critics said the rename did not change the product's premise — [Prothom Alo](https://en.prothomalo.com/corporate/unilever-to-rename-its-much-criticised-skin-cream-fair-lovely-as-glow-lovely). Status: opinion.

**Northeast Indians**
- Nido Tania, a student from Arunachal Pradesh, died after a racially charged assault in Delhi on 29 January 2014. The Home Ministry formed the M.P. Bezbaruah Committee, which reported on 11 July 2014 on discrimination, violence and policing failures faced by people from the Northeast — [The Week, Jan 2026](https://www.theweek.in/theweek/current/2026/01/23/from-anjel-chakma-to-nido-tania-why-india-keeps-failing-its-people-from-northeast.amp.html); [Policy Commons (report)](https://policycommons.net/artifacts/2283374/report-of-the-committee-under-the-chairmanship-of-shri-mp). Status: confirmed.
- The government (then Minister of State Kiren Rijiju) said all recommendations were implemented — [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/theprint-interview/govt-done-ensure-people-of-the-northeast-face-no-more-racism-rijiju/52620). Critics, including a committee member, say a dedicated anti-racial discrimination law was never enacted — [EastMojo, Jan 2026](https://eastmojo.com/premium/2026/01/15/from-nido-tania-to-anjel-chakma-did-the-bezbaruah-committee-fail/). Status: contested.
- Recent cases have renewed the debate: Anjel Chakma's death in Dehradun (reported Jan 2026), a fresh attack in Delhi raised by Tripura's Pradyot Manikya (March 2026), and the killing of a Manipuri musician (Sept 2026) — [The Week](https://www.theweek.in/magazine/theweek/current/2026/01/23/from-anjel-chakma-to-nido-tania-why-india-keeps-failing-its-people-from-northeast.amp.html); [India Today NE](https://www.indiatodayne.in/tripura/story/what-happened-to-bezbaruah-committee-report-pradyot-manikya-on-fresh-racial-attack-in-delhi-1357324-2026-03-10); [Newslaundry, 19 Sep 2026](https://www.newslaundry.com/2026/09/19/go-back-to-your-country-manipuri-musicians-killing-reignites-concerns-over-racism-against-northeasterners-and-govt-inaction). Status: reported. **Flag:** the descriptions in the search summary were brief. **Note:** an earlier summary called Pradyot Manikya "Tripura Chief Minister"; he is the TIPRA Motha founder, not chief minister. Verify his title before use.

**Africans in India**
- March 2017, Greater Noida: a mob attacked Nigerian students after a local teenager's death, which locals blamed on them; a Kenyan woman was also assaulted. Police booked about 600 people for rioting and 44 for attempted murder; seven were arrested — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/article/pti-stories/attacks-on-africans-xenophobic-racial-african-envoys-117040300581_1.html); [Anadolu](https://aa.com.tr/en/asia-pacific/african-students-in-india-to-hold-nationwide-protest/783043). Status: confirmed.
- The African Union Commission said it was "extremely appalled" by the "heinous attacks". The heads of African missions called them "xenophobic and racial" and said India took no "sufficient and visible deterring measures" — [Business Standard (AU)](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/news-ians/attacks-on-africans-in-india-appal-au-117033000795_1.html); [The Wire](https://cms.thewire.in/news/african-envoys-india-racism-un). The MEA called the attacks unacceptable but said it was "unfortunate" to call them racist before an investigation finished — [Scroll](https://scroll.in/latest/833638/scroll_in). Status: contested (characterisation disputed).

### Inferences
- Including internal prejudice inoculates the film against the charge of one-sidedness: the same dynamics (stereotype, mockery, colour and origin bias) operate inside India. That is a strong, fair mirror beat, but it needs neutral wording ("critics say", "the government says").

### Gaps
- No market-size figure for India's skin-lightening industry was found in this round. Do not quote one without a source.
- The Cisco case's status after 2023 is unknown.
- No systematic data (e.g. NCRB) on racially motivated crimes against Northeast Indians: India's crime statistics do not record race as a category (not verified this round).

## Q5. Counter-arguments to the "algorithm/racism" thesis, and platform policies

### Takeaway
Some critical content is legitimate journalism (pollution, scams, sanitation data above). Not every joke is racist, and the platforms' hate policies protect national origin and ethnicity, not criticism of a country's conditions. Enforcement is widely criticised as weak, and Meta loosened its policy in January 2025.

### Cited Findings
- YouTube says hate speech is not allowed, and its transparency report counts removals under the hate speech policy.
