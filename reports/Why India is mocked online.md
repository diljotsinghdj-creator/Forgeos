# Outrage, accents and algorithms target India

Online hostility toward Indians has grown sharply since late 2024. It rose in step with immigration and trade news (H-1B fights, tariff tensions, visa fees, migration flyers) and was spread by a small number of high-reach accounts on platforms that reward hostile engagement. Four monitoring groups with different methods all report the same **direction**: CSOH counted about **542,360 anti-South Asian hate posts on X in 18 months**, and NCRI counted **24,000+ anti-Indian posts with 300M+ views in 2025**. Their **magnitudes** cannot be compared. The mockery is built on three old layers that each start from something real and then stretch it to cover a whole people. The first is the stage "Indian voice", from Peter Sellers to Apu, with the call-centre and scam-call era added on top. The second is a hygiene trope that began on 4chan in 2015 and is now the fastest-growing category (+171%). The third, and the newest and largest, is a jobs-and-invasion narrative. Peer-reviewed research explains why filth clips travel: out-group language raises the odds of a share by about 67% per word, and Facebook once weighted the "angry" reaction at five times a like. The clip genre is also partly home-made and partly fake. Indian creators produce it, and fact-checkers have caught scripted, AI-generated and foreign footage passed off as India. The under-showing of India's space programme, payments system, wildlife and culture fits long-documented news values (negativity, elite-nation bias) and a colonial paper trail from Mill and Macaulay to Mayo's *Mother India*, more than any coordinated agenda. A neutral film has to carry the other side too. India's sanitation, air, river and food-safety problems are real and documented by India's own agencies. Scam call centres are real, and Indian police are the ones raiding them. India has its own internal prejudices of caste, colour and region. The research supports showing **timing, not cause**, between online spikes and offline attacks. Several numbers in the owner's draft need correcting before broadcast (see the corrections section).

**Status key used throughout:** **confirmed** means primary or official data, or several independent reliable outlets agree. **Reported** means a single monitoring group or outlet, or secondary coverage of a primary that was not opened. **Contested** means the sources disagree. **Opinion** means commentary or advocacy. **Method caveat:** network blocks stopped the researchers from opening most primary reports (CSOH, NCRI, IQAir, IC3, the UNESCO site and the IMF tables). Most figures therefore come from press coverage and search summaries. Every on-screen number needs a check against its primary source; the full list is in "Verify before broadcast" below.

## Four monitors, one direction: hate tracks the news cycle

The clearest answer to "why now" is that the hostility rises and falls with policy news that puts Indians in the frame. The first measured spike followed the **December 2024 H-1B fight on X**. Laura Loomer attacked the appointment of Sriram Krishnan as White House AI policy adviser, and Vivek Ramaswamy posted on 26 December that American culture "venerated mediocrity over excellence". Elon Musk defended the H-1B programme, and Donald Trump said he shared Musk's view *[confirmed]* ([CNBC, 27 Dec 2024](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/12/27/elon-musk-and-vivek-ramaswamy-open-rift-with-maga-loyalists-over-skilled-worker-visas.html); [The Hill](https://thehill.com/business/5060579-h-1b-visas-musk-maga-trump-ramaswamy-bannon-loomer-krishnan-immigration/)). The Center for the Study of Organized Hate (CSOH) then documented **128 X posts targeting Indians with 138.54 million views as of 3 Jan 2025**; **125 were still live** when the report came out on 9 Jan 2025 *[reported]* ([CSOH, 9 Jan 2025](https://www.csohate.org/2025/01/09/x-anti-indian-racism/); [CSOH press release](https://www.csohate.org/press-releases/new-report-surge-in-anti-indian-racism-and-xenophobia-on-x-after-krishnan-appointment-ramaswamy-tweet/)). How much of the wider backlash was racist rather than a policy objection is **contested**. CNBC called it "racially charged"; others said only a fringe made racist remarks. H-1B critics include labour advocates who argue about wages, not race. The film should keep **policy criticism of H-1B** separate from **ethnic hostility**.

CSOH's second report (16 Sep 2025) found **680 high-engagement anti-Indian posts with 281.2 million views between 1 Jul and 7 Sep 2025**. **121 posts (17.8%) used slurs** and drew 74.3M views. **August had nearly five times July's volume**, which CSOH links to the start of US–India tariff tensions; that link is CSOH's interpretation. Immigration and expulsion themes made up **nearly 70%** of the dataset *[reported]* ([CSOH, 16 Sep 2025](https://www.csohate.org/2025/09/16/anti-indian-racism-on-x-july-september-2025/); [American Bazaar, 18 Sep 2025](https://americanbazaaronline.com/2025/09/18/anti-indian-racism-on-x-on-the-rise-report-467757/)). Its largest report, "The Rising Tide of Anti-South Asian Hate in the United States" (28 Sep 2026), covers X, 4chan, 8chan and Gab. It counts about **542,360 anti-South Asian hate posts on X from Jan 2025 to Jun 2026**, with monthly volume up **240%** (12,904 in Jan 2024 to 43,843 in Jun 2026). **Five of the six highest months fell between Nov 2025 and Jun 2026.** About **45%** of posts framed South Asians as "invaders", "job thieves" or a demographic threat, and "pajeet" appeared in **nearly 30%**. **Hygiene-based dehumanisation was the fastest-growing category, up 171%.** CSOH also names **generative AI used to make racist caricatures** as a new amplifier *[reported]* ([CSOH, 28 Sep 2026](https://www.csohate.org/2026/09/28/anti-south-asian-hate-united-states-2026/); [Al Jazeera, 28 Sep 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/28/anti-south-asian-hate-speech-has-exploded-online-in-us-new-report-finds)). One flag: the 240% baseline (Jan 2024) falls outside the stated study window (from Jan 2025). Check this in the primary report before quoting it.

The Network Contagion Research Institute (NCRI) report "From Policy Drift to Purity Grift" (about Mar 2026) tracked **24,000+ anti-Indian posts on X in 2025 with 300M+ views**. Weekly volume **roughly tripled** against 2024 and peaked in **mid-December 2025 at 800+ posts a week** using slurs such as "pajeet" and "dothead". The spikes lined up with the DHS H-1B modernisation rule, State Department visa restrictions and the proposed **US$100,000 H-1B petition fee** *[reported]* ([NCRI](https://networkcontagion.us/reports/from-policy-drift-to-purity-grift-how-a-small-network-hijacked-the-immigration-debate/); [Business Standard, 12 Mar 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/immigration/indians-in-us-called-invaders-scammers-hate-posts-on-x-drew-300-mn-views-126031200623_1.html)). NCRI reports two findings that matter most for the film's "why". The first is **concentration**: three accounts drew **more than 10% of likes and about 20% of reposts**, and NCRI names Nick Fuentes and Sneako as amplifiers. The second is a **shift in rhetoric** from mid-2025, away from explicit slurs and toward "policy-framed" claims that Indian workers were "replacing" Americans. NCRI suggests this may be a way to evade moderation; that is NCRI's interpretation, not an established fact.

Moonshot data published by Stop AAPI Hate found that in Dec 2024–Jan 2025 **73% of anti-Asian slurs and 75% of anti-Asian threats** in the extremist spaces it monitors targeted South Asians. It called "pajeet" the **most-used anti-Asian slur** in those spaces, and it linked a summer-2025 spike ("rag head" +192%, "towelhead" +175%) partly to Zohran Mamdani's NYC mayoral campaign *[reported]* ([Stop AAPI Hate, 20 Feb 2025](https://stopaapihate.org/2025/02/20/post-election-surge-in-hate/); [Stop AAPI Hate, 7 Aug 2025](https://stopaapihate.org/2025/08/07/keeping-count-behind-the-spike-in-anti-south-asian-hate/)). Outside the US, the Institute for Strategic Dialogue (ISD) found anti-South Asian slur posts on X in Canada rose **more than 1,350% from 2023 to 2024**, framed around housing and job worries during the India–Canada diplomatic row *[reported]* ([ISD](https://www.isdglobal.org/digital-dispatch/the-rise-of-anti-south-asian-hate-in-canada/)). The July–September 2025 US dataset sometimes credited to ISD belongs to CSOH.

Why does policy news turn into ethnic hostility? Political science gives a better-supported answer than "they take our jobs". A peer-reviewed review by Hainmueller & Hopkins (2014) found **labour-market competition gets weak empirical support** as a driver of attitudes to immigration, while **sociotropic and cultural concerns** explain more *[confirmed]* ([Annual Review of Political Science, PDF](https://www.refugee-economies.org/assets/downloads/Journal_Hainmueller_and_Hopkins.pdf)). Their conjoint study found that Americans across parties **prefer highly educated migrants**. That is the opposite of what the H-1B hostility would suggest about public opinion, and it supports NCRI's "loud minority" reading *[confirmed]* ([ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/256025282_The_Hidden_American_Immigration_Consensus_A_Conjoint_Analysis_of_Attitudes_Toward_Immigrants)). Hopkins's "Politicized Places" (*APSR*, 2010) shows hostility appearing where a rapid local influx coincides with salient national threat rhetoric. In Journalist's Resource's summary of the study, when immigration is out of the headlines, residents of fast-changing counties are only **0.1%** more likely to want less immigration; during high national attention the gap is **9.9–15.1%** *[confirmed paper; figures reported]* ([Cambridge/APSR](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/american-political-science-review/article/abs/politicized-places-explaining-where-and-when-immigrants-provoke-local-opposition/2E1DEBDEA57191252E61B41084AFA348); [Journalist's Resource](https://journalistsresource.org/politics-and-government/politicized-provoke-opposition/)). Mutz (2018, *PNAS*) argued that **status threat** predicted 2016 voting better than personal economic hardship, but Stephen Morgan's reanalysis disputes it *[contested]* ([ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/324708462_Status_threat_not_economic_hardship_explains_the_2016_presidential_vote); [Morgan reanalysis](https://statmodeling.stat.columbia.edu/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/Morgan-Fake-News-Mutz-Reanalysis-20180510.pdf)). Taken together, these studies suggest a chain the film can state: people of Indian origin became **visible in high-status roles**, national rhetoric framed them as a threat, and a few amplifiers turned that into volume. Present this as what the research suggests, not as something proven for this case.

### Offline incidents: show the timing, not a cause

Offline incidents happened in the same window, and official data in some countries rose too. No source shows that online posts **caused** any specific attack, so the film must present **co-occurrence in time only**. Canada has the clearest official signal. **Police-reported hate crimes against South Asians rose 15% to 321 in 2024 and to 483 in 2025 (+49%)** *[confirmed; StatCan counts include suspected incidents]* ([StatCan Daily, 30 Mar 2026](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/260330/dq260330a-eng.htm); [StatCan table, 22 Jul 2026](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/260722/t006a-eng.htm)). In the US, preliminary 2025 FBI data analysed by Brian Levin show a **record 177 anti-Sikh** incidents and **28 anti-Hindu** incidents while overall hate crimes fell *[reported, preliminary]* ([Axios, 9 Apr 2026](https://axios.com/2026/04/09/hate-crime-data-latinos-sikhs-2025)). The US has no "anti-Indian" category. The official numbers run to tens or hundreds while the online counts run to hundreds of thousands, so the film must not equate online volume with crime.

In Ireland, an Indian man was assaulted in Tallaght on 19 Jul 2025 and **Gardaí recorded it as racially motivated**. The Indian Embassy issued a safety advisory on 1 Aug 2025, and Justice Minister Jim O'Callaghan said racist attacks "won't be tolerated" *[confirmed]* ([Irish Times, 26 Jul 2025](https://www.irishtimes.com/crime-law/2025/07/26/he-kept-saying-what-wrong-have-i-done-why-me-an-indian-man-is-left-stripped-and-bloodied-on-an-irish-street); [TheJournal.ie](https://www.thejournal.ie/indian-embassy-in-ireland-issues-safety-advisory-after-recent-attacks-6780443-Aug2025/)). In Australia, flyers for the **31 Aug 2025 "March for Australia"** rallies singled out Indian migration, and the government condemned the rallies *[confirmed; crowd sizes contested]* ([Al Jazeera, 1 Sep 2025](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/9/1/clashes-in-australias-melbourne-as-thousands-rally-against-immigration)). In the UK, West Midlands Police treated the rapes of two British Sikh women, in Oldbury (Sep 2025) and Walsall (Oct 2025), as **racially aggravated** *[confirmed]* ([Express & Star, 26 Oct 2025](https://www.expressandstar.com/uk-news/2025/10/26/rape-of-woman-in-walsall-being-treated-as-racially-aggravated-police-say)). MEA data reported by ThePrint list **14 attacks on Indians in the US in 2025**, the most of any country *[reported; "attacks" is not the same as confirmed hate crimes]* ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/diplomacy/14-attacks-on-indians-reported-in-us-in-2025-highest-in-the-world-27-more-than-2024-mea-data/3013668/)). The **Dallas killing of motel manager Chandra Nagamallaiah (10 Sep 2025)** must **not** be presented as anti-Indian hate. Investigators have identified no racial motive, and the case became material for a separate immigration argument *[killing and charge confirmed; motive not established]* ([Deseret News, 19 Sep 2025](https://www.deseret.com/politics/2025/09/19/chandra-nagamallaiah-murder-trump-made-a-comment-on-illegal-immigration)).

## The accent joke was performed first, then fused with the scam call

The mocked "Indian voice" has a traceable media lineage, and much of it was performed by non-Indian actors. Peter Sellers played an Indian doctor in brownface in *The Millionairess*, and its promotional single "Goodness Gracious Me" (with Sophia Loren) **reached No. 4 in the UK in 1960** *[confirmed]*. He then played Hrundi V. Bakshi in *The Party* (1968), which PBS calls the film's "inescapable and enduring problem" *[casting confirmed; characterisation opinion]* ([Official Charts, 19 Jan 1961](https://www.officialcharts.com/charts/singles-chart/19610119/7501); [PBS American Masters](https://www.pbs.org/wnet/americanmasters/this-caricature-was-once-celebrated-by-a-generation-starving-for-representation/33933)). Hank Azaria voiced Apu on *The Simpsons* from 1989/90 and has said he based Apu partly on Sellers' Bakshi *[reported]*. Hari Kondabolu's 2017 documentary *The Problem with Apu* argued that the voice became a playground taunt. The show replied on 8 Apr 2018 with Lisa's line that something "applauded and inoffensive is now politically incorrect" *[confirmed]* ([Time, Apr 2018](https://time.com/5232896/the-simpsons-respond-apu-controversy); [The Ringer, 9 Apr 2018](https://www.theringer.com/tv/2018/4/9/17215174/the-simpsons-hari-kondabolu-problem-with-apu-response)). Azaria stepped away in **January 2020**. In **April 2021** he apologised and called "Apu" "practically a slur at this point" *[confirmed]* ([Global News, Apr 2021](https://globalnews.ca/news/7754709/apu-hank-azaria-simpsons-voice-apology/amp)).

The second layer came from outsourcing. Indian call-centre institutes taught a "neutral accent" stripped of "regional biases" *[confirmed, 2003 reporting]* ([The Tribune, 14 Jul 2003](https://www.tribuneindia.com/2003/20030714/login/main9.htm)). In 2025 Teleperformance began rolling out Sanas's AI "accent translation" to soften Indian agents' accents *[reported]* ([CX Today](https://cxtoday.com/contact-center/teleperformance-uses-ai-to-soften-indian-call-center-agents-accents-and-so-does-its-competition)). A real criminal industry grew up alongside the legitimate one. Examples include the **Mira Road IRS-impersonation raids (Oct 2016)** *[raids confirmed; scale contested]* ([Business Standard/PTI, Apr 2017](https://www.business-standard.com/article/pti-stories/fake-call-centre-scam-mastermind-arrested-in-mumbai-117040800226_1.html)) and the CBI's **Operation Chakra** series from **October 2022**, with Chakra-II (Oct 2023) running 76 searches on Microsoft and Amazon referrals *[confirmed]* ([The Record](https://therecord.media/india-scam-tech-support-call-centers-raids-amazon-microsoft)). IC3 put US tech-support fraud losses at about **$1.46B in 2024** and **$2.13B in 2025** *[reported; primary PDF not opened]* ([AARP](https://www.aarp.org/money/scams-fraud/fbi-ftc-report-2025-losses.html)). **IC3 does not attribute these losses to India**, so the film must not imply that it does. The accent joke merges millions of legitimate BPO workers with a criminal subset into one "tech support" figure. No study was found that measures that fusion directly, so it is an inference.

Linguistics research treats accent bias as social rather than linguistic. Lippi-Green's *English with an Accent* (1997; 2nd ed. 2012) describes a "standard language ideology", and her study of Disney films found foreign-accented characters disproportionately cast as villains *[confirmed per reviews; check counts in the book]* ([CSUF review PDF](https://english.fullerton.edu/publications/clnArchives/pdf/wynnEng.pdf)). Timming (2017, *Work, Employment and Society*) found US managers rated **Indian-accented** applicants lower, especially for customer-facing roles *[confirmed]* ([St Andrews portal](https://research-portal.st-andrews.ac.uk/en/publications/the-effect-of-foreign-accent-on-employability-a-study-of-the-aura/)). The famous Lev-Ari & Keysar (2010) finding that accented statements are believed less **has failed replication** and must be presented as disputed if used *[contested]* ([replication PDF](https://escholarship.org/content/qt8hd9v4ff/qt8hd9v4ff.pdf)). The premise of the joke is that the accent signals poor English. The 2011 Census undercuts it: about **129 million Indians (≈10.6%) reported speaking English** *[reported; self-report, not fluency]* ([Census of India 2011](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/43470)), and Kachru's World Englishes framework treats Indian English as a norm-developing variety *[model confirmed; "legitimacy" framing is the school's opinion, disputed by Quirk]* ([Purdue OWL](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/multilingual/world_englishes)). The in-group complication is that Russell Peters defends his imitations of his parents' accents as "just being true to what I'm talking about" *[opinion]* ([Daily Beast](https://thedailybeast.com/russell-peters-defends-comedic-stereotypes-nothing-hacky-about-indian-accent)), while South Asian critics say such routines gave others licence to repeat "the voice" *[opinion]* ([Yahoo News](https://news.yahoo.com/mindy-kaling-other-south-asian-215721954.html)).

## Filth clips spread because outrage is the currency platforms pay in

"India is dirty" clips combine three features that peer-reviewed research links to engagement: they name an out-group, they invite moral disgust, and they are negative. **Rathje, Van Bavel & van der Linden (PNAS, June 2021)** analysed **2.73 million** Facebook and Twitter posts. Each out-group word raised the **odds of a share by about 67%**, several times the effect of negative or moral-emotional words, and out-group posts drew more "angry" reactions *[confirmed; US politics, observational]* ([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8256037/)). Brady et al. (PNAS 2017) found about **20% more retweets per moral-emotional word**, though a later estimate puts it nearer 12% *[reported; effect size contested]* ([NYU CSMaP](https://csmapnyu.org/research/academic-research/emotion-shapes-the-diffusion-of-moralized-content-in-social-networks); [Tech Policy Press](https://techpolicy.press/psychologists-observe-moral-contagion-on-facebook-twitter)). Robertson et al. (*Nature Human Behaviour*, 2023) used **105,000+ randomised headline tests** and found negative words raise clicks *[confirmed]* ([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10202797/)). None of these studies is about India, so applying them to hygiene clips is an inference.

Ranking systems turned those reactions into reach. According to the Facebook Papers, from 2017 Facebook's feed **weighted every emoji reaction, including "angry", at five times a like**. Its own data scientists confirmed in 2019 that angry-reaction posts were disproportionately likely to contain misinformation, toxicity and low-quality news. The angry weight was later cut, to 4x in 2018 and to zero by the end of 2020 *[reported, from internal documents]* ([Washington Post via MediaWell, 26 Oct 2021](https://mediawell.ssrc.org/news-items/five-points-for-anger-one-for-a-like-how-facebooks-formula-fostered-rage-and-misinformation-the-washington-post/); [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/emoji-reactions-cute-addition-facebook-became-headache-rcna3747)). The counter-view should be included: Techdirt argued the framing was misleading, and Cato noted "angry" was the least-used reaction *[opinion]* ([Cato](https://www.cato.org/blog/washington-post-facebook-promotes-outrage-isnt-outrageous)). The same Haugen disclosures show a moderation gap. A test account posing as a Kerala user (from 4 Feb 2019) followed recommendations for three weeks and its feed filled with hate and violence. Much of that content went "never flagged or actioned" because Facebook lacked **classifiers in Hindi and Bengali**. Facebook says it added Hindi classifiers in 2018 and Bengali in 2020, which is a company claim *[reported; Facebook's response contested]* ([Al Jazeera, 25 Oct 2021](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2021/10/25/facebook-india-hate-speech-misinformation-muslims-social-media); [AP via NBC Washington](https://www.nbcwashington.com/news/tech/facebook-dithered-in-curbing-divisive-user-content-in-india-2/2848890/)). That content was mostly communal and anti-Pakistan hate inside India, not foreign "dirty India" content. Use it as evidence of how ranking and language gaps work, not as evidence about hygiene videos. Money adds to the incentive. Since **October 2024**, X pays creators based on **engagement from Premium users** rather than on ads *[confirmed]* ([TechCrunch, 9 Oct 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/09/x-changes-creator-payouts-to-depend-on-engagement-not-ads/)). YouTube and TikTok rank on watch time and interactions, but they publish no weights for negative reactions, so **no 5x equivalent may be claimed for them** *[reported]* ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-provides-new-overview-of-how-its-video-recommendation-systems-work/)).

The supply side is large and not only foreign. YouTube says it paid **more than ₹21,000 crore to Indian creators, artists and media companies in three years** (Neal Mohan, 1 May 2025) *[confirmed as YouTube's claim]* ([YouTube blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/neal-mohan-creator-economy-waves-2025/)). Indian creators publish videos such as "I Tried India's Most Unhygienic Street Food" *[reported]* ([Recapio digest](https://recapio.com/digest/i-tried-indias-most-unhygienic-street-food-shocking-results-by-sahilxp)). Foreign vloggers' "dirty street food" and slum videos are a recognised and much-criticised genre: examples include German vlogger Christian Betzmann (Jan 2023) and an Australian's "rich to poor in 2 seconds" video (Jul 2025) *[reported]* ([Business Today, Jan 2023](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/trends/story/german-influencer-abuses-indian-street-hawkers-gets-schooled-358694-2023-01-03); [Business Today, Jul 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/latest/economy/story/this-country-can-go-rich-to-poor-in-2-seconds-aussies-brutal-india-video-goes-viral-484155-2025-07-11)). The "poverty porn" critique goes back at least to *Slumdog Millionaire* (NPR, 5 Mar 2009) *[debate confirmed; critique opinion]* ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2009/03/05/101487893/poverty-porn-education-or-exploitation)). A measurable portion of the footage is not real India at all. **Correctiv (13 May 2026)** found viral Indian food "disgust" videos to be **AI-generated** ([Correctiv](https://correctiv.org/faktencheck/2026/05/13/ekel-fuer-klicks-diese-videos-von-essen-in-indien-sind-ki-generiert/)). BOOM traced a "toilet cleaner in pani puri" clip to scripted 2022 content and showed that a "dishes washed in pothole water" video was **not from India** ([BOOM](https://www.boomlive.in/fact-check/politics/fake-news-viral-video-zubair-muslim-man-using-toilet-cleaner-to-cook-pani-puri-scripted-video-dramatised-video-factcheck-18508); [BOOM](https://www.boomlive.in/video-of-restaurant-staff-washing-dishes-with-pothole-water-is-not-from-india)). The Quint caught an AI "sewage water" clip passed off as real *[all confirmed fact-checks]* ([The Quint](https://www.thequint.com/news/webqoof/ai-video-of-man-adding-sewage-water-to-food-viral-as-real-fact-check)). Several staged clips first circulated in India with **communal** captions before being reused abroad as "India is dirty" content, so the film should keep the domestic and foreign audiences distinct. **No study measures what share of India content shows filth, or what share of viral hygiene clips is staged.** The film should not state either percentage.

## The other India is under-shown by news values more than by design

The structural explanation is the most defensible one. Galtung & Ruge (1965) showed that the more distant and less "elite" a nation is, the more negative an event must be before it becomes news. Harcup & O'Neill's retest (2017) found "bad news" still a core news value *[confirmed]* ([SAGE](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/002234336500200104); [Taylor & Francis](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/1461670X.2016.1150193)). Soroka, Fournier & Nir (*PNAS*, 2019) measured stronger physiological reactions to negative news across **17 countries**, so audiences drive the skew as well as editors *[confirmed]* ([PNAS](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1908369116)). India-specific "media agenda" studies are thin or written from an advocacy position, and the popular "Western media bashes India" claims are op-eds *[opinion]* ([ThePrint op-ed](https://theprint.in/opinion/nyt-to-time-magazine-western-media-loves-to-bash-modis-india-china-could-be-the-culprit/1101209)). Binyavanga Wainaina's satire "How to Write About Africa" (*Granta* 92, 2005) shows that the same pattern applies to other regions, which answers the objection that this is "just anti-India bias" *[confirmed; satire, present as opinion]* ([Granta](https://granta.com/how-to-write-about-africa/)).

The "backward India" frame also has a dated paper trail. James Mill wrote *The History of British India* (1817) without visiting India *[confirmed]* ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/James-Mill)). **Macaulay's Minute on Indian Education (2 Feb 1835)** says "a single shelf of a good European library was worth the whole native literature of India and Arabia", while admitting "I have no knowledge of either Sanscrit or Arabic" *[confirmed; check punctuation against a scholarly edition]* ([Columbia, Pritchett](http://www.columbia.edu/itc/mealac/pritchett/00generallinks/macaulay/txt_minute_education_1835.html)). Katherine Mayo's *Mother India* (1927) drew Gandhi's reply calling it "the report of a drain inspector sent out with the one purpose of opening and examining the drains of the country", a line that names the technique of the modern filth clip *[quote confirmed; Young India date reported]* ([mkgandhi.org](https://www.mkgandhi.org/letters/others/mayo.htm)). Alfred Sauvy coined "Third World" on 14 Aug 1952 to mean non-aligned, not poor *[confirmed]* ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Third-World)). Said's *Orientalism* (1978) supplies the critique, though his main cases are Arab and Islamic, not Indian.

## The other side: real problems, home-grown clips and internal prejudice

A neutral film must show that the stereotype exaggerates problems that are real, and that India's own agencies document them. NFHS-5 (2019–21) found **19% of households had no toilet facility**, even though India was declared open-defecation-free on 2 Oct 2019 on the basis of self-declarations. The same survey series shows large gains in access *[confirmed; indicators contested]* ([Deccan Herald, May 2022](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/19-households-do-not-use-any-toilet-facility-nfhs-survey-1108334.html); [Rajya Sabha PQ 257, 1 Aug 2022](https://rsdebate.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/731536/1/PQ_257_01082022_U1691_p390_p391.pdf)). IQAir's 2025 report ranks India **6th most polluted of 143 countries (PM2.5 48.9 µg/m³)**, with Loni first among the world's cities and Delhi fourth. The country's rank has improved from 3rd in 2023 *[reported]* ([The Wire](https://thewire.in/environment/a-2025-world-air-quality-report-lists-india-as-the-sixth-most-polluted-country)). During the Maha Kumbh (Feb 2025) the CPCB told the NGT that water at Prayagraj was **not fit for bathing because of faecal coliform**; the government disputed the readings *[confirmed filing; contested]* ([Newslaundry, 18 Feb 2025](https://www.newslaundry.com/amp/story/2025%2F02%2F18%2Fmahakumbh-waters-unsafe-for-bathing-a-report-finds-faecal-bacteria-13-times-over-safe-limit)). FSSAI tested **170,513 samples in 2023-24 and found 19.82% non-conforming**, of which 6,782 were unsafe. Non-conforming is not the same as adulterated, and another Lok Sabha reply gives a different total *[confirmed; totals contested between documents]* ([Lok Sabha PDF](https://fcainfoweb.nic.in/PMS/writereaddata/2024_LS_B_2538.pdf)). Waste processing is disputed: a minister told Parliament 73% is processed, while CPCB 2020-21 data put it at about 47% *[contested]* ([FactChecker](https://www.factchecker.in/context-check/does-india-really-process-73-of-its-waste-as-union-minister-claims-829905)).

The scam stereotype has a real core, concentrated in the NCR. In December 2025 the CBI, acting on FBI inputs, raided a Noida centre. The FBI tied **$48.7M** in losses from 600+ US victims to the network, while the CBI estimated about $8.5M *[contested; allegations only]* ([The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/india/cbi-busts-transnational-cybercrime-racket-in-noida-with-fbi-help-us-embassy-hails-us-india-partnership-1503526840.html)). The fair counterweight comes from **Microsoft's 2021 survey**: Indians had the **world's highest tech-support-scam encounter rate (69%)**, and 48% of Indian respondents kept engaging with the scammer against a 16% global average. Indians are major victims as well as the place where the scams are located *[confirmed]* ([Microsoft India](https://news.microsoft.com/en-in/seven-out-of-10-consumers-in-india-encountered-tech-support-scams-in-the-past-year-microsoft-survey/)). Indian media also produce sensational content. A Newslaundry analysis found **416 complaints to the NBDSA since 2019 resulted in just 12 fines** *[reported; Newslaundry's own analysis]* ([Newslaundry, 5 Dec 2025](https://www.newslaundry.com/2025/12/05/5-years-416-complaints-just-12-fines-the-mute-button-of-indian-news-tvs-first-self-regulator)), and the NBDSA fined Zee News Rs 1 lakh over an unverified viral video *[confirmed]* ([The Federal](https://thefederal.com/category/news/zee-news-fined-fake-viral-video-journalism-crisis-discussion-230692)).

Internal prejudice gives the film a fair mirror, but each case needs both positions quoted. On caste, Seattle banned caste discrimination in Feb 2023. California's SB 403 was **vetoed by Governor Newsom in Oct 2023** as "unnecessary". Equality Labs and Dalit groups backed the bill; the Hindu American Foundation opposed it as singling out Hindus *[confirmed]* ([Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/legal-ops-and-tech/newsom-vetoes-californias-first-in-nation-ban-on-caste-bias)). The status of the Cisco caste case after 2023 is **unconfirmed**. On colour, Hindustan Unilever renamed Fair & Lovely as **Glow & Lovely on 2 Jul 2020**; critics said the product premise did not change *[confirmed; critique opinion]* ([Scroll](https://scroll.in/latest/966333/scroll_in)). People from India's Northeast have faced racist violence since Nido Tania's death in Delhi (29 Jan 2014) and the Bezbaruah Committee report (11 Jul 2014). The government says it implemented the recommendations; critics say no anti-racial-discrimination law was passed *[contested]* ([EastMojo, Jan 2026](https://eastmojo.com/premium/2026/01/15/from-nido-tania-to-anjel-chakma-did-the-bezbaruah-committee-fail/)). After mob attacks on Nigerian students in Greater Noida (Mar 2017), African envoys called them "xenophobic and racial", while the MEA said it was "unfortunate" to call them racist before an investigation finished *[contested characterisation]* ([Scroll](https://scroll.in/latest/833638/scroll_in); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/article/pti-stories/attacks-on-africans-xenophobic-racial-african-envoys-117040300581_1.html)). Set beside the MEA's August 2025 statement that it had "strongly taken up" attacks on Indians in Ireland ([Tribune India](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/world/have-strongly-taken-up-this-matter-with-irish-authorities-in-dublin-mea-on-cases-of-violence-against-indians-in-ireland)), this gives the film a factual juxtaposition. It should be presented with both statements quoted, not as an accusation. The fair dividing line on policy is this: YouTube's hate-speech policy protects national origin and ethnicity, not criticism of a country's conditions, and it allows educational and documentary use (EDSA) *[confirmed]* ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/intl/en_be/howyoutubeworks/our-commitments/standing-up-to-hate)). Criticising Delhi's air is journalism; saying Indians are dirty is a stereotype.

## Conclusion

The research reframes the question. "Why do people hate Indians online?" has less to do with Indians than with **visibility, timing and incentives**. Indians became conspicuous in exactly the high-status, policy-sensitive roles (tech visas, executive appointments, elected office) that political science predicts will draw threat rhetoric. A handful of accounts converted each news event into volume, and the platforms' payment and ranking systems treat an outraged reply the same as an approving one. The genuinely new element in 2025–26 is not the slur, which dates from 2015. It is the **disguise**: hostility moving into "policy" language, and synthetic AI footage filling the hygiene genre with clips that never happened.

For the film, the strongest stance is also the most neutral one: **concede the drains, then show the selection**. India's own NFHS, CPCB, FSSAI and CBI records give the critics their real numbers. Gandhi's 1927 "drain inspector" line already names the technique of reporting only the drains. The film's power comes from putting the same verified rigour on both sides of the frame, the sewage readings and the soft landing at 69°S. It should not swap one curated India for another.
